Common name: Lollipop Climber, Shivalingi, Native Bryony, Striped Cucumber
Scientific name: Diplocyclos palmatus (L.) C.Jeffrey
Family: Cucurbitaceae

Habitat: A vine found in rainforests and dry rainforests, thickets, monsoon forests, and lowland to upland disturbed areas.

Biodiversity Value: In Marathi it is called shivlingi because its seed resembles a lingam, giving it cultural and devotional significance in parts of India.
