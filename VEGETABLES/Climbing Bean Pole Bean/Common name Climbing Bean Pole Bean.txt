Common name: Climbing Bean / Pole Bean
Scientific name: Phaseolus vulgaris
Family: Fabaceae
Type: Annual climbing herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm fields and gardens with fertile soil and trellis support.
Features: Twining stems, trifoliate leaves, pea flowers, and long pods containing beans.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers attract bees and roots fix nitrogen through symbiotic bacteria.
Economic importance: Important vegetable and pulse crop eaten as green pods or mature beans.
Medicinal importance: Properly cooked beans are food; raw beans can be harmful and should not be consumed.
