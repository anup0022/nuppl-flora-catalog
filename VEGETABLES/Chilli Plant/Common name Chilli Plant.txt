Common name: Chilli Plant
Scientific name: Capsicum annuum
Family: Solanaceae
Type: Short-lived perennial herb grown as an annual
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm sunny fields, pots, and kitchen gardens with fertile well-drained soil.
Features: Branched stems, simple leaves, small white flowers, and hollow fruits ranging from green to red, yellow, or orange.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide pollen for bees and fruits feed birds that disperse seeds.
Economic importance: Major spice and vegetable crop used fresh, dried, pickled, and processed.
Medicinal importance: Capsaicin-rich fruit is used traditionally; concentrated preparations can irritate skin and digestion.
