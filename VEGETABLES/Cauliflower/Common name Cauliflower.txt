Common name: Cauliflower
Scientific name: Brassica oleracea var. botrytis
Family: Brassicaceae
Type: Annual or biennial leafy herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season vegetable crop in fertile, evenly moist soil.
Features: Upright leaves surround a compact white, cream, or coloured head made of immature flower branches.
Biodiversity Value: Bolted plants provide yellow flowers visited by bees and other insects.
Economic importance: Widely cultivated vegetable eaten fresh, cooked, pickled, or processed.
Medicinal importance: Nutritious food; medical claims should not replace clinical care.
