Common name: Radish
Scientific name: Raphanus sativus
Family: Brassicaceae
Type: Annual or biennial root herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season fields and gardens with loose well-drained soil.
Features: Basal lobed leaves and a swollen edible root in white, red, pink, or black forms; flowers are usually white or lilac.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide nectar and pollen for bees and other insects.
Economic importance: Fast-growing root vegetable eaten raw, cooked, pickled, and as leafy greens.
Medicinal importance: Traditional food-based uses exist; medicinal doses require professional guidance.
