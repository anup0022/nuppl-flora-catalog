Common name: Carrot
Scientific name: Daucus carota subsp. sativus
Family: Apiaceae
Type: Biennial root herb grown as an annual
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season crop in loose deep soil with full sun and regular moisture.
Features: Finely divided leaves and a swollen taproot in orange, red, purple, yellow, or white cultivars; flowers form umbels in the second year.
Biodiversity Value: Umbel flowers attract bees, hoverflies, and other beneficial insects.
Economic importance: Major root vegetable eaten fresh, cooked, juiced, and processed.
Medicinal importance: Nutritious food rich in carotenoids; concentrated preparations need professional guidance.
