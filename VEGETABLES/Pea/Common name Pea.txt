Common name: Pea
Scientific name: Pisum sativum
Family: Fabaceae
Type: Annual climbing herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season fields and gardens with fertile soil, sun, and support for climbing varieties.
Features: Compound leaves end in tendrils; white or pink pea flowers produce pods containing edible seeds.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers feed bees and root nodules fix nitrogen in soil.
Economic importance: Important vegetable and pulse crop eaten fresh, dried, or processed.
Medicinal importance: Nutritious food source; therapeutic use requires evidence and medical guidance.
