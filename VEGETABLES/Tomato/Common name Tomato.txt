Common name: Tomato
Scientific name: Solanum lycopersicum
Family: Solanaceae
Type: Annual or short-lived perennial herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cultivated in sunny fields, gardens, and containers with fertile well-drained soil.
Features: Branched aromatic stems, compound leaves, yellow flowers, and fleshy berries in many shapes and colours.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide pollen for buzz-pollinating bees.
Economic importance: Major vegetable and food crop eaten fresh, cooked, dried, and processed.
Medicinal importance: A nutritious food source; unripe leaves and stems should not be eaten.
