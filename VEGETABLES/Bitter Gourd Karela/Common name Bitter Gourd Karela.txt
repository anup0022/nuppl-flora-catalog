Common name: Bitter Gourd / Karela
Scientific name: Momordica charantia
Family: Cucurbitaceae
Type: Annual climbing vine
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm tropical fields and gardens with fertile soil, sun, water, and trellis support.
Features: Tendrilled vine, lobed leaves, separate yellow flowers, and ridged bitter fruits that turn orange when mature.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide nectar for bees and other pollinators.
Economic importance: Important vegetable, pickling crop, and traditional food plant.
Medicinal importance: Used traditionally in food-based preparations; concentrated use may affect blood sugar and requires medical advice.
