Common name: Barbati / Boda / Yardlong Bean
Scientific name: Vigna unguiculata subsp. sesquipedalis
Family: Fabaceae
Type: Annual climbing herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm-season crop grown on supports in sunny fertile gardens and fields.
Features: Twining stems, trifoliate leaves, pale flowers, and very long tender pods.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers support bees and the legume improves soil nitrogen.
Economic importance: Popular vegetable eaten as tender pods and a useful smallholder crop.
Medicinal importance: Nutritious food; beans should be properly prepared before eating.
