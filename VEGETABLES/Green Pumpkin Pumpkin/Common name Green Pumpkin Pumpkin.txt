Common name: Green Pumpkin / Pumpkin
Scientific name: Cucurbita moschata
Family: Cucurbitaceae
Type: Annual trailing vine
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm sunny fields and kitchen gardens with fertile soil, space to trail, and regular water.
Features: Coarse hairy vines, large lobed leaves, separate yellow male and female flowers, and ribbed fruits.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers are important nectar and pollen resources for bees.
Economic importance: Fruits, flowers, and seeds are widely used as food and livestock feed.
Medicinal importance: Pumpkin is a nutritious food; concentrated remedies need professional advice.
