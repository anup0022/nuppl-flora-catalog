Common name: Arbi / Taro
Scientific name: Colocasia esculenta
Family: Araceae
Type: Perennial herb grown as a food crop
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm humid fields, wetlands, and gardens with rich moist soil; cultivars vary in water tolerance.
Features: Large heart-shaped leaves arise from underground corms; flowering is uncommon in cultivated forms.
Biodiversity Value: Dense foliage provides shelter for small animals and moist-soil invertebrates.
Economic importance: Corms and leaves are important food crops when properly cooked.
Medicinal importance: Raw corms and leaves contain irritating calcium oxalates and must be thoroughly cooked.
