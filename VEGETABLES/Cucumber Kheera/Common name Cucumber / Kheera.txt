Common name: Cucumber / Kheera
Scientific name: Cucumis sativus
Family: Cucurbitaceae
Type: Annual trailing or climbing vine
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm sunny fields and gardens with fertile soil, regular water, and support where needed.
Features: Rough vines, tendrils, yellow male and female flowers, and elongated watery fruits.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide pollen and nectar for bees.
Economic importance: Major fresh vegetable and pickling crop.
Medicinal importance: Fruit is a hydrating food; therapeutic use should be medically assessed.
