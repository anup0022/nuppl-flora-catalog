Common name: Cabbage
Scientific name: Brassica oleracea var. capitata
Family: Brassicaceae
Type: Biennial leafy herb grown as an annual
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season crop grown in fertile moist fields and kitchen gardens.
Features: Broad overlapping leaves form a dense compact head; mature plants produce yellow four-petaled flowers.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers support bees and other small pollinators when plants are allowed to bolt.
Economic importance: Important leafy vegetable eaten raw, cooked, fermented, and preserved.
Medicinal importance: Nutritious food rich in fibre and vitamins; not a substitute for medical treatment.
