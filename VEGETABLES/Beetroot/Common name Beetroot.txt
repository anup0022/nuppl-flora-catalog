Common name: Beetroot
Scientific name: Beta vulgaris
Family: Amaranthaceae
Type: Biennial root herb grown as an annual
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season crop in loose fertile soil where the swollen root can develop.
Features: Basal leaves and a swollen red, purple, golden, or striped storage root; flowering occurs in the second year.
Biodiversity Value: Mature flowers support small pollinating insects.
Economic importance: Root and leaves are eaten fresh, cooked, pickled, or processed as food colour.
Medicinal importance: A nutritious vegetable; concentrated beet products should be used with medical advice when relevant.
