Bellyache Bush

Also known as: Bellyache Bush, Black Physicnut, Cotton-leaf Physicnut

KingdomPlantae
FamilyEuphorbiaceae
GenusJatropha
SpeciesJatropha gossypiifolia
Scientific nameJatropha gossypiifolia L.
CategoryShrubs
IUCN Red ListLeast Concern (LC) — IUCN Red List LC

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Mexico, South America and the Caribbean islands, now spread throughout the tropics; grows up to 2 m tall as a shrub with reddish-purple young foliage maturing to green, small red flowers with yellow centres, and cherry-sized poisonous seed pods. Naturalized in northern Australia (declared a pest plant in Queensland) and a declared noxious weed in Puerto Rico.

Biodiversity Value

  • Coloured foliage and red flowers attract pollinating insects; toxic parts deter browsing by livestock and wildlife.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Grown ornamentally for its foliage, but classed as an invasive weed in several countries due to prolific, toxic seeding.

Medicinal Importance

  • Widely used across traditional and veterinary medicine systems (known locally in India as jarak merah or sibidigua) for antihypertensive, anti-inflammatory, antipyretic, antimicrobial and wound-healing purposes; also studied for biodiesel and pesticide potential, though the seeds and latex are toxic and should be handled with care.
