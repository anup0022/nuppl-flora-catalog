Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Moist sunny or partly shaded garden beds and disturbed soils during the warm rainy season.
  • Upright succulent stems, lance-shaped leaves, showy flowers, and explosive seed capsules.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide nectar for bees and butterflies.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Seasonal bedding plant traditionally grown in home gardens.

Medicinal Importance

  • Traditional topical uses are reported, but safe dosage and identification require professional guidance.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
