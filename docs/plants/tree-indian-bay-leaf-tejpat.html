Indian Bay Leaf Tejpat

Also known as: Indian Bay Leaf, Tejpat, Malabar Leaf

KingdomPlantae
FamilyLauraceae
GenusCinnamomum
SpeciesCinnamomum tamala
Scientific nameCinnamomum tamala (Buch.-Ham.) T.Nees & C.H.Eberm.
CategoryTrees

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A tree native to northern India (Assam and the Western Himalayas), Bangladesh, Nepal, Myanmar, Laos, Vietnam and southwestern China, growing in Himalayan foothill forests.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • The aromatic leaves, called tejpat or tejpatta, are a staple spice in Indian, Nepali and Bangladeshi cuisine, imparting a strong cassia-like aroma quite distinct from Mediterranean bay laurel, with which it is often confused and mislabelled; historically thought to be a major source of the classical spice "malabathrum".

Medicinal Importance

  • Leaves contain aromatic compounds including linalool and eugenol, and extracts have been studied for antidiarrhoeal, antifungal and antioxidant activity in traditional and experimental medicine.
