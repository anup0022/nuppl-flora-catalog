Spinach Palak

Also known as: Spinach / Palak

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAmaranthaceae
GenusSpinacia
SpeciesSpinacia oleracea
Scientific nameSpinacia oleracea
TypeAnnual leafy herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cool-season vegetable grown in fertile moist beds and fields with regular irrigation.
  • Basal rosette of tender green leaves; mature plants produce slender flowering stems.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide pollen for small insects when plants are allowed to mature.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Widely cultivated leafy vegetable eaten fresh or cooked.

Medicinal Importance

  • A nutritious food source; therapeutic use should not replace medical care.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
