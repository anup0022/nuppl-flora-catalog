Vegetable Plants Specimen

Cabbage

KingdomPlantae
FamilyBrassicaceae
GenusBrassica
SpeciesBrassica oleracea
Scientific nameBrassica oleracea var. capitata
TypeBiennial leafy herb grown as an annual
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cool-season crop grown in fertile moist fields and kitchen gardens.
  • Broad overlapping leaves form a dense compact head; mature plants produce yellow four-petaled flowers.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers support bees and other small pollinators when plants are allowed to bolt.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Important leafy vegetable eaten raw, cooked, fermented, and preserved.

Medicinal Importance

  • Nutritious food rich in fibre and vitamins; not a substitute for medical treatment.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
