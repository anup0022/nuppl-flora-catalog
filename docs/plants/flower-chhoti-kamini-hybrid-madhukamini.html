Chhoti Kamini Hybrid Madhukamini

Also known as: Chhoti Kamini / Hybrid Madhukamini

KingdomPlantae
FamilyRutaceae
GenusMurraya
SpeciesMurraya paniculata
Scientific nameMurraya paniculata
TypeEvergreen flowering shrub
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cultivated in warm gardens and clipped hedges with sun to partial shade.
  • Compact glossy foliage and repeated flushes of small fragrant white flowers.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers support bees and other small pollinators.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Used as a compact fragrant hedge and ornamental shrub.

Medicinal Importance

  • No medicinal recommendation is made for this cultivated hybrid form.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
