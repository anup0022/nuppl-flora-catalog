Crabgrass

Also known as: Crabgrass, Hairy Crabgrass, Hairy Finger-grass

KingdomPlantae
FamilyPoaceae
GenusDigitaria
SpeciesDigitaria sanguinalis
Scientific nameDigitaria sanguinalis (L.) Scop.
CategoryGrasses

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • An annual grass known nearly worldwide as a common weed of lawns, roadsides and disturbed ground, with an inflorescence of long thin radiating branches, often reddish or purplish.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Cultivated by Slavic peoples in Eastern Europe during the Middle Ages for grain, cooked in soups and porridges, earning it the name "Polish millet". Brought to the United States by immigrants as a hand-foraged grain and used as animal fodder and hay; relatively high in protein compared with other grasses. Considered a difficult-to-control lawn weed today.
