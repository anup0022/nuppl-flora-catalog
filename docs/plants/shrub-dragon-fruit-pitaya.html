Dragon Fruit Pitaya

Also known as: Dragon Fruit / Pitaya

KingdomPlantae
FamilyCactaceae
GenusSelenicereus
SpeciesSelenicereus undatus
Scientific nameSelenicereus undatus
TypeClimbing cactus
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Tropical and subtropical gardens and farms; grows on supports in warm climates with porous soil and good drainage.
  • Triangular succulent stems climb using aerial roots; large white night-blooming flowers develop into pink-skinned fruit.

Biodiversity Value

  • Night flowers provide nectar for moths and bats; fruits feed birds and mammals.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Commercial fruit crop and ornamental climber valued for edible pitaya.

Medicinal Importance

  • Fruit is nutritious food; medicinal claims require appropriate clinical evidence.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
