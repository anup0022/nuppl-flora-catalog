Banana (Kela)

Also known as: Banana / Kela

Scientific nameMusa spp.
FamilyMusaceae
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Southeast Asia. Cultivated worldwide in tropics
  • Very common in UP including Kanpur area in your coordinates. Grown in home gardens and as a commercial crop
  • Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate. Needs warm, humid weather and no frost
  • Needs full sun, deep, fertile, well-drained loamy soil with lots of organic matter. Cannot tolerate waterlogging
  • Grows 2-6 m tall in clumps. Has huge, oblong leaves with parallel veins. Old dried leaves hang down like in your photo

Economic Importance

  • Food: Fruit eaten fresh or cooked. Green bananas used as vegetable. Leaves used as plates and for cooking/packaging food. Rich in potassium, Vitamin B6 and C

Medicinal Importance

  • Fruit, leaves, and pseudostem used traditionally for digestion, wounds, and as an antacid. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
  • Economic: One of the most important fruit crops in India. Provides quick income - fruits in 9-12 months
  • Cultural: Considered auspicious. Leaves used in religious ceremonies and festivals

Field Notes

  • Common cultivated species: Musa paradisiaca or Musa × paradisiaca
  • Pseudostem used as cattle fodder. Fiber from stem used for ropes and handicrafts. Flower bud called "banana heart" is eaten as vegetable
