Grasses Specimen

Lemongrass

Also known as: Lemongrass / Nibu Ghaas / Hari Chai Patti

KingdomPlantae
GenusCymbopogon
SpeciesCymbopogon citratus
Scientific nameCymbopogon citratus or Cymbopogon flexuosus – both are cultivated in North India
TypePerennial herb, grass family Poaceae
CategoryGrasses

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical Asia (India, Sri Lanka, Southeast Asia), widely cultivated across India. Thrives in hot, humid tropical to subtropical climate at 25-40°C; sensitive to frost, dying back in severe winter but regrowing from roots. Needs full sun for strong aroma and oil content (minimum 6 hours daily), getting weak in shade. Prefers well-drained sandy loam to loamy soil, tolerating poor soil but not waterlogging, and growing well even in marginal land. Planted in home gardens, tea gardens, field bunds, and commercial plantations; Odisha, Assam, Kerala, and UP are major producing states. A perennial clump that can be harvested multiple times a year, with the rainy season giving the best growth.

Biodiversity Value

  • Planted on field boundaries and slopes to prevent soil erosion, with dense roots holding soil, and repels some insects from crops.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Culinary: Fresh leaves and lower stem used to flavour tea ("lemongrass chai"), soups, curries, and Thai dishes, giving a strong lemon-like flavour without acidity.
  • Essential oil: Leaves steam-distilled for lemongrass oil rich in citral, used in perfumes, soaps, cosmetics, mosquito repellents, and aromatherapy; India is a major exporter.
  • Agriculture: Planted on field boundaries and slopes to prevent soil erosion, with dense roots holding soil, and repels some insects from crops.
  • Commercial crop: Low input, high value crop for small farmers, harvested every 3-4 months, sold fresh in local markets or to distilleries.
  • Household: Dried leaves used as potpourri; oil used in floor cleaners and air fresheners for lemon scent.

Medicinal Importance

  • In Ayurveda and folk medicine, tea made from leaves is used for fever, cold, cough, digestive issues, and as a mild sedative; the oil has antibacterial and antifungal properties. Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use.
