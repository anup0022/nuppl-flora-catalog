Saptaparni Devil Tree

Also known as: Saptaparni / Devil Tree

KingdomPlantae
FamilyApocynaceae
GenusAlstonia
SpeciesAlstonia scholaris
Scientific nameAlstonia scholaris
TypeEvergreen tree
CategoryTrees

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to South and Southeast Asia; grows in moist tropical forests, roadsides, gardens, and institutional grounds. Tolerates warm lowland conditions and well-drained soil.
  • Tall straight trunk, whorled branches, glossy oblong leaves, milky latex, and fragrant greenish-white flowers in clusters.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide nectar for insects and the dense crown offers nesting cover for birds.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Planted as a shade and avenue tree; wood is used for light construction, boxes, and plywood.

Medicinal Importance

  • Bark and leaves are used in traditional medicine, but medicinal use should be supervised by a qualified professional.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Least Concern (LC) - IUCN Red List
