Smooth Agave

Also known as: Smooth Agave / Smooth Century Plant / Agave desmetiana

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsparagaceae
GenusAgave
SpeciesAgave desmetiana
Scientific nameAgave desmetiana
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Mexico, widely grown as an ornamental in India and common in urban gardens. Grown in pots, rock gardens, and nurseries. Thrives in hot, dry, tropical to subtropical climate, needing well-drained sandy soil, full sun, and being extremely drought tolerant. Forms a stemless succulent rosette, 60-90 cm tall and wide, with smooth, spineless, blue-green to bright green, strap-like leaves with soft flexible tips and no marginal teeth.

Biodiversity Value

  • Extremely low water needs, good for dry gardens and erosion control.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental: Very popular landscape plant, used in xeriscaping, pots, gardens, and corporate landscaping; the smooth, spineless leaves make it safer for homes than other agaves.
  • Other uses: Leaf fibres used for rope and handicrafts in native areas; some varieties used for making alcoholic beverages like agave syrup.
  • Commercial: High demand in the nursery trade, propagating easily from pups/offsets.

Medicinal Importance

  • Sap from Agave species is used in traditional Mexican medicine for wound care and skin irritation, similar to aloe.
