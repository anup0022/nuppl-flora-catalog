Smooth Agave

Also known as: Smooth Agave / Smooth Century Plant / Agave desmetiana

Scientific nameAgave desmetiana
FamilyAsparagaceae
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Mexico. Widely grown as ornamental in IndiaCommon in UP including Kanpur area. Grown in pots, rock gardens, and nurseries like in your photoThrives in hot, dry, tropical to subtropical climateNeeds well-drained sandy soil and full sun. Extremely drought tolerantStemless succulent rosette, 60-90 cm tall and wide. Leaves are smooth, spineless, blue-green to bright green, strap-like with soft flexible tips. No teeth on margins. This matches your photo - clean, smooth, upright leaves in a symmetrical rosette

Economic Importance

  • Ornamental: Very popular landscape plant. Used in xeriscaping, pots, gardens, and corporate landscaping. The smooth, spineless leaves make it safer for homes vs other agaves
  • Extremely low water needs. Good for dry gardens and erosion controlCommercial: High demand in nursery trade. Propagates easily from pups/offsets

Medicinal Importance

Medicinal notes pending.

Field Notes

  • Leaf fibers used for rope and handicrafts in native areas. Some varieties used for making alcoholic beverages like agave syrup
