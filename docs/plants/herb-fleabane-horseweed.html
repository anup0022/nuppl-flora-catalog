Fleabane Horseweed

Also known as: Fleabane, Horseweed, Canadian Horseweed

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsteraceae
GenusErigeron
SpeciesErigeron canadensis
Scientific nameErigeron canadensis L. (syn. Conyza canadensis)
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Erigeron is a large genus of around 460 species in the daisy family with a cosmopolitan distribution, most diverse in North America. Erigeron canadensis (horseweed) is an erect, well-branched annual weed with small white to pale ray flowers, common on disturbed and cultivated ground, roadsides and waste places worldwide.

Biodiversity Value

  • Wind-dispersed seeds let it rapidly colonise bare and disturbed ground as an early pioneer species.
  • Small flower heads provide late-season nectar and pollen for various insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Regarded mainly as a widespread agricultural weed; some other Erigeron species are cultivated as ornamental garden plants for their daisy-like flowers.
