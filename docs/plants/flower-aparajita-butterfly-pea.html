Aparajita Butterfly Pea

Also known as: Aparajita / Butterfly Pea

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusClitoria
SpeciesClitoria ternatea
Scientific nameClitoria ternatea
TypePerennial climbing herb
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm open gardens and hedges; grows in full sun or light shade with support and moderate moisture.
  • Twining stems, compound leaves, vivid blue or white pea-shaped flowers, and flat pods.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers attract bees and other pollinators; the legume fixes nitrogen.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental climber, traditional food and beverage colourant, and garden nitrogen fixer.

Medicinal Importance

  • Used traditionally in herbal preparations; clinical and dosage advice is required.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
