Vegetable Plants Specimen

Green Pumpkin Pumpkin

Also known as: Green Pumpkin / Pumpkin

KingdomPlantae
FamilyCucurbitaceae
GenusCucurbita
SpeciesCucurbita moschata
Scientific nameCucurbita moschata
TypeAnnual trailing vine
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm sunny fields and kitchen gardens with fertile soil, space to trail, and regular water.
  • Coarse hairy vines, large lobed leaves, separate yellow male and female flowers, and ribbed fruits.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers are important nectar and pollen resources for bees.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Fruits, flowers, and seeds are widely used as food and livestock feed.

Medicinal Importance

  • Pumpkin is a nutritious food; concentrated remedies need professional advice.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
