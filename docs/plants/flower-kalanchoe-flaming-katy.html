Kalanchoe Flaming Katy

Also known as: Kalanchoe / Flaming Katy

KingdomPlantae
FamilyCrassulaceae
GenusKalanchoe
SpeciesKalanchoe blossfeldiana
Scientific nameKalanchoe blossfeldiana
TypeSucculent perennial herb
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Sunny windowsills, containers, and dry warm garden beds with excellent drainage.
  • Fleshy scalloped leaves and dense clusters of red, pink, orange, yellow, or white flowers.

Biodiversity Value

  • Open flowers provide nectar for small insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Popular long-flowering pot plant and indoor ornamental.

Medicinal Importance

  • Some Kalanchoe species are toxic; do not consume this ornamental plant.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
