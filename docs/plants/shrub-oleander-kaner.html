Oleander (Kaner)

Also known as: Oleander / Kaner / Lal Kaner / Safed Kaner

KingdomPlantae
GenusNerium
SpeciesNerium oleander
Scientific nameNerium oleander
TypeEvergreen flowering shrub
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Mediterranean region and parts of Asia. Widely naturalized and cultivated across India, very common in UP including the Kanpur region in your coordinates
  • Thrives in hot, dry tropical and subtropical climate. Tolerates extreme heat up to 45°C and mild frost. Evergreen in UP winters
  • Prefers full sun for profuse flowering. Tolerates partial shade but flowers less
  • Grows in almost any soil - sandy, rocky, loamy, or even poor soil. Highly drought-tolerant and pollution-tolerant
  • Planted in home gardens, parks, road dividers, highway medians, and temples. Also grows wild along riverbanks and wastelands
  • Flowers nearly year-round in warm climate, peak in summer and monsoon

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental: Primary use. Grown for showy flowers in pink, white, red, or yellow. Extensively used in landscaping, highway medians, and public gardens because it’s hardy and blooms heavily with zero care
  • Environmental: Excellent for urban greening. Tolerates air pollution, dust, and vehicle exhaust. Used as a noise and dust barrier along roads

Medicinal Importance

  • In Ayurveda and folk medicine, root and leaves used externally for skin diseases and leprosy. Cardiac glycosides like oleandrin are extracted for heart drugs. Important: All parts are highly toxic if ingested. Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use. Never self-medicate
  • Cultural: Flowers offered in temples. Associated with Lord Shiva in some regions
  • Pesticidal: Leaf extract used as bio-pesticide against some insects
