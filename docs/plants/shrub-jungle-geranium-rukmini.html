Shrubs Specimen

Jungle Geranium (Rukmini)

Also known as: Ixora / Jungle Geranium / Rangan / Rukmini

KingdomPlantae
FamilyRubiaceae
GenusIxora
SpeciesIxora coccinea
Scientific nameIxora coccinea
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical Asia. Widely cultivated across India
  • Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown in home gardens, pots, parks, and temple gardens like in your photo
  • Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate
  • Needs well-drained, slightly acidic soil and full sun to partial shade
  • Evergreen shrub, 1-2 m tall. Leaves are glossy, leathery, opposite, with prominent veins. Flowers grow in dense round clusters. In your photo it’s the red variety with small 4-petaled star-shaped flowers

Biodiversity Value

  • Attracts butterflies and pollinators.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental: One of the most popular flowering shrubs in India. Blooms almost year-round in warm climates. Flowers come in red, orange, yellow, pink, and white. Used for landscaping, borders, hedges, and potted plants
  • Medicinal: Roots, leaves, and flowers used in traditional medicine for dysentery, ulcers, skin diseases, and fever. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
  • Religious/Cultural: Flowers used in temple offerings and garlands

Medicinal Importance

  • Roots, leaves and flowers used in traditional medicine for dysentery, skin diseases and fever.
