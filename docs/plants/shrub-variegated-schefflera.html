Variegated Schefflera

Also known as: Variegated Schefflera / Dwarf Umbrella Plant / Variegated Octopus Tree

Scientific nameSchefflera arboricola 'Variegata'
FamilyAraliaceae
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Taiwan and Hainan. Widely cultivated across India
  • Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown in pots, nurseries, offices, and home gardens like in your photo
  • Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate
  • Needs well-drained soil and bright indirect light to partial shade. Tolerates indoor conditions well
  • Grows 1-3 m tall. Leaves are palmately compound with 7-9 leaflets arranged like an umbrella. This matches your photo - glossy, variegated leaves with green and creamy-yellow patches growing in multiple pots

Economic Importance

  • Ornamental: One of the most popular indoor and patio plants in India. The variegated form is especially valued for bright foliage. Used for interior decoration, balconies, and landscaping
  • Air purifying: Listed by NASA for removing common indoor air pollutants like benzene and formaldehyde
  • Commercial: Huge demand in nursery and interior landscaping trade. Easy to propagate from cuttings

Medicinal Importance

Medicinal notes pending.

Field Notes

  • Low maintenance and tolerant to neglect. Makes a good bonsai subject
