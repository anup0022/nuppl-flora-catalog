Flowering / Ornamental Specimen

Yellow Rain Lily

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAmaryllidaceae
GenusZephyranthes
SpeciesZephyranthes citrina
Scientific nameZephyranthes citrina
TypeBulbous perennial herb
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Sunny lawns, borders, and pots; flowering often follows rain in warm climates.
  • Narrow grass-like leaves arise from bulbs and bright yellow funnel-shaped flowers open singly.

Biodiversity Value

  • Open flowers provide nectar for small pollinating insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Popular low-maintenance ornamental for borders, rock gardens, and containers.

Medicinal Importance

  • Bulbs should not be consumed; no medicinal use is recommended.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
