Bonpland's Croton Ban Tulsi

Also known as: Bonpland's Croton, Ban Tulsi

KingdomPlantae
FamilyEuphorbiaceae
GenusCroton
SpeciesCroton bonplandianus
Scientific nameCroton bonplandianus Baill.
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A member of the large genus Croton within the spurge family, common as a weed of waste ground, roadsides and cultivated land across the Indian subcontinent. Like other Croton species, it is a hardy annual or short-lived shrub with simple alternate leaves and small inconspicuous flowers.

Biodiversity Value

  • Inconspicuous flowers are wind- and insect-pollinated; tolerates poor soil and readily colonises disturbed ground.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Used in local folk medicine in parts of India; otherwise mainly significant as a common roadside and farmland weed.

Medicinal Importance

  • Used in local folk medicine in parts of India for minor skin ailments and fever.
