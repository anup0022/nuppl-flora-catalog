Trees Specimen

Weeping Fig

Also known as: Weeping Fig, Benjamin Fig

KingdomPlantae
FamilyMoraceae
GenusFicus
SpeciesFicus benjamina
Scientific nameFicus benjamina L.
CategoryTrees

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to South and Southeast Asia and Australia, reaching up to 30 m tall in the wild with gracefully drooping branchlets and glossy oval leaves; grown widely as a shade and avenue tree in tropical and subtropical regions, and as a popular houseplant in temperate climates due to its tolerance of low light and poor growing conditions.

Biodiversity Value

  • Small figs are an important food source for birds and fruit bats where it grows wild.
  • Depends on a specific fig-wasp species for pollination, a tightly co-evolved plant-insect relationship.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • One of the most common ornamental and indoor foliage trees worldwide, planted in parks, along roads and as bonsai; capable of some indoor air purification of gaseous formaldehyde, though its invasive root system can damage pavements and foundations.
