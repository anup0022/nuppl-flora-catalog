Vegetable Plants Specimen

Carrot

KingdomPlantae
FamilyApiaceae
GenusDaucus
SpeciesDaucus carota
Scientific nameDaucus carota subsp. sativus
TypeBiennial root herb grown as an annual
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cool-season crop in loose deep soil with full sun and regular moisture.
  • Finely divided leaves and a swollen taproot in orange, red, purple, yellow, or white cultivars; flowers form umbels in the second year.

Biodiversity Value

  • Umbel flowers attract bees, hoverflies, and other beneficial insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Major root vegetable eaten fresh, cooked, juiced, and processed.

Medicinal Importance

  • Nutritious food rich in carotenoids; concentrated preparations need professional guidance.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
