Herbs Specimen

Mose Rose (Gul-e-Dupheri)

Also known as: Moss Rose / Portulaca / 9 O'Clock Flower / Gul-e-Dupheri

KingdomPlantae
FamilyPortulacaceae
GenusPortulaca
SpeciesPortulaca grandiflora
Scientific namePortulaca grandiflora
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to South America. Widely cultivated across India
  • Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown in gardens, pots, hanging baskets, and as groundcover like in your photo
  • Thrives in hot, tropical to subtropical climate
  • Needs well-drained soil and full sun. Very drought tolerant
  • Low-growing succulent herb, 10-20 cm tall, spreading. Leaves are small, fleshy, cylindrical/needle-like, arranged in whorls. Stems are reddish and spreading. This matches your photo with the bright green needle leaves

Biodiversity Value

  • Good for soil erosion control and xeriscaping because it needs very little water
  • Attracts bees and butterflies.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental: One of the most popular summer bedding plants. Produces colorful flowers in red, pink, yellow, orange, white that open in morning sun. Blooms from March to November
  • Medicinal: Leaves and whole plant used in traditional medicine for skin problems, inflammation, and as a cooling agent. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
  • Other uses: Edible leaves used in salads in some places. Good for soil erosion control and xeriscaping because it needs very little water

Medicinal Importance

  • Whole plant used in traditional medicine as a cooling agent for skin inflammation, similar to its relative purslane.
