Thorn Apple Jimsonweed

Also known as: Thorn Apple / Jimsonweed / Dhatura / Safed Dhatura

KingdomPlantae
GenusDatura
SpeciesDatura stramonium
Scientific nameDatura stramonium
TypeAnnual herb, family Solanaceae
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to North America, now naturalized as a cosmopolitan weed. Found across India in wastelands, roadsides, and disturbed areas. Common in UP including the Kanpur area in your coordinatesThrives in hot, dry tropical to warm temperate climate. Grows aggressively in UP summer and monsoon at 20-38°C. Killed by frostNeeds full sun – minimum 6 hours daily. Grows poorly in shadePrefers well-drained, sandy to loamy soil. Tolerates poor, nutrient-deficient, and alkaline soils. Common in dry, neglected plots like in your photo. Does not tolerate waterloggingLife cycle 4-6 months. Germinates with monsoon, flowers Aug-Oct, sets seed by Nov-Dec, then dies in winter

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • White trumpet flowers open at night and are pollinated by moths. Thorny seed pods deter animalsToxic weed: All parts – leaves, flowers, seeds – are poisonous to humans and livestock. Accidental poisoning common in children due to attractive flowers and spiny fruits. No fodder value

Medicinal Importance

  • Major source of tropane alkaloids – atropine, hyoscyamine, and scopolamine. Used in modern medicine for asthma inhalers, pre-anesthetic medication, motion sickness patches, and to dilate pupils. Note: All parts are highly toxic. Self-medication is dangerous and can be fatal. Consult a healthcare professional – this plant is for pharma industry use onlyAyurveda: Used in very controlled, processed forms as Dhatura for asthma, pain, and skin diseases after shodhana purification. Unprocessed plant is poisonCommercial: Leaves and seeds collected for pharmaceutical companies. India exports Datura extractReligious: Offered to Lord Shiva in Hindu worship, especially during Shravan and Mahashivratri
