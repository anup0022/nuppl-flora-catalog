Vegetable Plants Specimen

Radish

KingdomPlantae
FamilyBrassicaceae
GenusRaphanus
SpeciesRaphanus sativus
Scientific nameRaphanus sativus
TypeAnnual or biennial root herb
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cool-season fields and gardens with loose well-drained soil.
  • Basal lobed leaves and a swollen edible root in white, red, pink, or black forms; flowers are usually white or lilac.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide nectar and pollen for bees and other insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Fast-growing root vegetable eaten raw, cooked, pickled, and as leafy greens.

Medicinal Importance

  • Traditional food-based uses exist; medicinal doses require professional guidance.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
