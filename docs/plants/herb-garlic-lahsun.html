Herbs Specimen

Garlic Lahsun

Also known as: Garlic / Lahsun

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAmaryllidaceae
GenusAllium
SpeciesAllium sativum
Scientific nameAllium sativum
TypeBulbous perennial herb grown as an annual crop
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cultivated in cool, dry-season fields and gardens with full sun and loose well-drained soil.
  • Narrow strap-like leaves arise from a compound bulb; a mature scape bears a rounded flower cluster.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers, when produced, attract small pollinators.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Important culinary bulb, spice, preservative, and crop used worldwide.

Medicinal Importance

  • Used traditionally as food and a household remedy; concentrated supplements may interact with medicines.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
