Herbs Specimen

Sicklepod Tora

Also known as: Sicklepod, Tora, Sickle Senna

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusSenna
SpeciesSenna tora
Scientific nameSenna tora (L.) Roxb.
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Grows wild throughout most of the tropics and is considered a weed in many places, though its native range is thought to be Central America. Closely related and often historically confused with Senna obtusifolia.

Biodiversity Value

  • Yellow flowers attract bees; pods split and scatter seed widely, letting it establish quickly on disturbed ground.
  • As a legume, it can improve nitrogen levels in the soils it grows in.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Its seeds (commonly called "tora" or "chakvad" seeds in India) have traditional use as a coffee substitute and in folk food and medicine preparations across South and Southeast Asia.
