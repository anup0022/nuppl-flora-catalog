Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Moist shaded soil, stone, bark, and compacted surfaces where humidity remains high.
  • Small leafy shoots form green cushions; spores develop in capsules on slender stalks.

Biodiversity Value

  • Moss mats retain moisture, provide microhabitat for invertebrates, and help stabilize fine soil.
