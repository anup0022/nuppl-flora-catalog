Herbs Specimen

Shatavari

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsparagaceae
GenusAsparagus
SpeciesAsparagus racemosus
Scientific nameAsparagus racemosus
TypePerennial climbing herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to South Asia; grows in scrub, woodland margins, and well-drained soils in warm climates.
  • Slender climbing stems carry fine needle-like cladodes and produce fragrant white flower racemes; roots are fleshy and tuberous.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers support pollinating insects and the climbing growth contributes to understory diversity.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Cultivated and collected as an Ayurvedic medicinal herb.

Medicinal Importance

  • Roots are traditionally used as a tonic, but dosage and identity must be confirmed by a qualified practitioner.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
