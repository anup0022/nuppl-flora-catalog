Vegetable Plants Specimen

Cucumber Kheera

Also known as: Cucumber / Kheera

KingdomPlantae
FamilyCucurbitaceae
GenusCucumis
SpeciesCucumis sativus
Scientific nameCucumis sativus
TypeAnnual trailing or climbing vine
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm sunny fields and gardens with fertile soil, regular water, and support where needed.
  • Rough vines, tendrils, yellow male and female flowers, and elongated watery fruits.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide pollen and nectar for bees.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Major fresh vegetable and pickling crop.

Medicinal Importance

  • Fruit is a hydrating food; therapeutic use should be medically assessed.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
