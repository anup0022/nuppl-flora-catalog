Herbs Specimen

Indian Borage Cuban Oregano

Also known as: Indian Borage / Cuban Oregano

Scientific namePlectranthus amboinicus
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • It is a perennial herb (not a tree), belonging to the mint family.
  • Grows as a shrub-like herb with fleshy, aromatic leaves.
  • Native to Southeast Asia and Africa, but widely cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions.
  • Thrives in warm climates, often found in home gardens, pots, and open soil.

Economic Importance

  • Leaves are used as a culinary herb for flavoring dishes, similar to oregano.
  • Traditionally valued in Ayurveda and folk medicine for cough, cold, asthma, and digestive issues.
  • Extracts are studied for antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.
  • Also grown as an ornamental plant due to its attractive foliage and easy maintenance.

Medicinal Importance

Medicinal notes pending.
