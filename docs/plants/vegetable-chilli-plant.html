Vegetable Plants Specimen

Chilli Plant

KingdomPlantae
FamilySolanaceae
GenusCapsicum
SpeciesCapsicum annuum
Scientific nameCapsicum annuum
TypeShort-lived perennial herb grown as an annual
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm sunny fields, pots, and kitchen gardens with fertile well-drained soil.
  • Branched stems, simple leaves, small white flowers, and hollow fruits ranging from green to red, yellow, or orange.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide pollen for bees and fruits feed birds that disperse seeds.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Major spice and vegetable crop used fresh, dried, pickled, and processed.

Medicinal Importance

  • Capsaicin-rich fruit is used traditionally; concentrated preparations can irritate skin and digestion.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
