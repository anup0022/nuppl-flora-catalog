Pineapple Plant

KingdomPlantae
FamilyBromeliaceae
GenusAnanas
SpeciesAnanas comosus
Scientific nameAnanas comosus
TypePerennial fruit herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Tropical crop plant grown in warm sunny fields and home gardens with porous, well-drained soil.
  • Stiff spiny rosette leaves surround a compact flowering stalk that develops into a crowned compound fruit.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide nectar for insects and the dense rosette offers small-animal shelter.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Important fruit crop used fresh, preserved, juiced, and processed into food products and fibre.

Medicinal Importance

  • Fruit is nutritious, but concentrated preparations should be used with professional advice.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
