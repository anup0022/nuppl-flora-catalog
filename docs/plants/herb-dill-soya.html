Herbs Specimen

Dill Soya

Also known as: Dill / Soya

KingdomPlantae
FamilyApiaceae
GenusAnethum
SpeciesAnethum graveolens
Scientific nameAnethum graveolens
TypeAnnual aromatic herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cultivated in sunny beds and kitchen gardens in cool to warm seasons; prefers fertile, well-drained soil.
  • Finely divided leaves, hollow stems, and yellow umbrella-shaped flower clusters produce aromatic seeds.

Biodiversity Value

  • Umbel flowers attract bees, hoverflies, and other beneficial insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Leaves and seeds are used as culinary herbs, pickles, and spice.

Medicinal Importance

  • Traditionally used as a digestive herb; concentrated preparations require professional guidance.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
