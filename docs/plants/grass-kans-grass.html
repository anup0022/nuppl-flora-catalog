Kans Grass

Also known as: Kans Grass, Wild Sugarcane

KingdomPlantae
FamilyPoaceae
GenusSaccharum
SpeciesSaccharum spontaneum
Scientific nameSaccharum spontaneum L.
CategoryGrasses

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native throughout much of tropical and subtropical Asia, northern Australia, and eastern and northern Africa. A perennial grass growing up to three metres tall with spreading rhizomatous roots.

Biodiversity Value

  • Extensive rhizomes bind loose river-bank and floodplain soil, reducing erosion during monsoon floods.
  • Provides cover and nesting habitat for birds and small wildlife in riverine grasslands.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • A wild relative of cultivated sugarcane; it has hybridized with Saccharum officinarum to help produce cultivated sugarcane varieties such as Saccharum barberi and Saccharum sinense.
