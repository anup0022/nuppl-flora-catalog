Madhukamini Badi Kamini

Also known as: Madhukamini / Badi Kamini

KingdomPlantae
FamilyRutaceae
GenusMurraya
SpeciesMurraya paniculata
Scientific nameMurraya paniculata
TypeEvergreen flowering shrub
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm gardens, hedges, and roadsides in well-drained soil with sun or partial shade.
  • Glossy compound leaves, dense branching, fragrant white flowers, and small red berries.

Biodiversity Value

  • Fragrant flowers attract bees, butterflies, and night-flying pollinators; berries feed birds.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Widely planted as a fragrant hedge, specimen shrub, and bonsai subject.

Medicinal Importance

  • Traditional uses exist in some regions; this catalog does not recommend self-medication.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
