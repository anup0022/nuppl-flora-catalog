Rajnigandha Tuberose

Also known as: Rajnigandha / Tuberose

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsparagaceae
GenusAgave
SpeciesAgave amica
Scientific nameAgave amica (syn. Polianthes tuberosa)
TypeBulbous perennial herb
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm sunny gardens and flower fields with fertile, well-drained soil.
  • Narrow basal leaves and tall spikes bearing waxy, intensely fragrant white flowers.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide nectar for moths, bees, and other pollinators.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Important cut flower used in bouquets, garlands, worship, and perfumery.

Medicinal Importance

  • Fragrant flowers are used traditionally, but no self-medication is recommended.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
