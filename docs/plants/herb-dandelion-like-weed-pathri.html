Herbs Specimen

Dandelion-like weed(Pathri)

Also known as: Creeping Launaea / Alarka / Pathri / Dandelion-like weed

KingdomPlantae
GenusLaunaea
SpeciesLaunaea procumbens
Scientific nameLaunaea procumbens (also Launaea aspleniifolia)
TypeAnnual or perennial herb in the Asteraceae family
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to India, North Africa, and West Asia
  • Very common across UP, including Kanpur. Grows in lawns, fields, roadsides, and wastelands
  • Thrives in full sun and dry to moderately moist conditions
  • Tolerates poor, sandy, rocky, and alkaline soils. Common in disturbed ground
  • Grows from winter through summer. Produces yellow flowers that turn into white, fluffy seed heads like dandelions
  • Drought tolerant with a deep taproot

Biodiversity Value

  • Important weed for pollinators.
  • Yellow flowers attract bees and butterflies.
  • Seeds feed small birds

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Medicinal: Used in Ayurveda and folk medicine. The whole plant is considered cooling and used for skin diseases, rheumatism, jaundice, and as a diuretic. Latex applied on wounds. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
  • Fodder: Eaten by goats and sheep when young. Older plants become bitter
  • Edible: Young tender leaves eaten as leafy vegetable in some regions after cooking. Rich in minerals
  • Weed: Can be invasive in lawns and crops, but usually not a major agricultural pest
  • Indicator plant: Often grows in overgrazed or nutrient-poor soils
