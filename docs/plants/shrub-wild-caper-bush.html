Wild Caper Bush

Also known as: Wild Caper Bush, Hedge Caper

KingdomPlantae
FamilyCapparaceae
GenusCapparis
SpeciesCapparis sepiaria
Scientific nameCapparis sepiaria L.
CategoryShrubs
IUCN Red ListLeast Concern (LC) — IUCN Red List LC

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A prickly, evergreen shrub growing 3-5 m tall with white flowers appearing from February onwards, found across dry deciduous forests, foothills and scrub jungles with a pantropical distribution spanning Africa, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, India and Australia; recorded in numerous Indian regions including Kolhapur, Dharwad, Mysore and Kerala.

Biodiversity Value

  • An important dry-season fodder source for wildlife such as chital, sambar and nilgai in Rajasthan; its bark serves as a larval host plant for the great orange-tip and yellow orange-tip butterflies of South India.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • An important dry-season fodder source for wildlife such as chital, sambar and nilgai in Rajasthan; its bark serves as a larval host plant for the great orange-tip and yellow orange-tip butterflies of South India.

Medicinal Importance

  • Flowers and fruit of this and related Capparis species have traditional use as food and folk medicine, including as a cooling agent and in oral health remedies documented among tribal communities of Rajasthan and Karnataka.
