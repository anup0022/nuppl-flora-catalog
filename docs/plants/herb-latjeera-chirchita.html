Latjeera Chirchita

Also known as: Latjeera, Chirchita, Prickly Chaff Flower, Devil's Horsewhip

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAmaranthaceae
GenusAchyranthes
SpeciesAchyranthes aspera
Scientific nameAchyranthes aspera L.
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A wild, perennial, erect herb distributed throughout the tropical world; can be found growing as an introduced species and common weed, and is invasive in some Pacific Island environments.

Biodiversity Value

  • Small greenish flowers are insect- and wind-pollinated; hooked seed spikes cling to fur and clothing for wide dispersal.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Long used in traditional medicine across South Asia and Africa; otherwise mainly a common roadside and wasteland weed.

Medicinal Importance

  • Long used in folk medicine across India and Australia. Historical records describe the herb given in India for dropsy, seeds used for hydrophobia and snake-bites as well as ophthalmia and skin diseases, and fresh leaf pulp applied externally to scorpion stings; ash of the plant was also used in washing clothes as a source of potash.
