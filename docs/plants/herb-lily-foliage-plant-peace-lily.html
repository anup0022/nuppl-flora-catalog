Lily Foliage Plant Peace Lily

Also known as: Lily Foliage Plant, Peace Lily

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAraceae
GenusSpathiphyllum
SpeciesSpathiphyllum genus
Scientific nameSpathiphyllum sp. (genus Spathiphyllum Schott)
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A genus of 59 species of evergreen herbaceous perennials native to tropical regions of the Americas and southeastern Asia, growing on shady, moist forest floors along rivers and streams. Dark green, glossy elliptical to lanceolate leaves emerge in rosettes from a low creeping stem, with a distinctive white or greenish-white spathe surrounding the flowering spadix.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Widely grown worldwide as a popular houseplant valued for its attractive dark foliage, contrasting white flowers and tolerance of low light and infrequent watering; commercially propagated by tissue culture for the interior plantscaping trade.
