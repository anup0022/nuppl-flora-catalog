KingdomPlantae

Scientific nameCyperus niveus Retz.
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A sedge native to parts of Africa, the Middle East and Asia, including India.

Biodiversity Value

  • A sedge of open, often damp ground; its seed heads provide food for small birds and insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Little direct commercial use; mainly significant as ground cover on wastelands and disturbed sites.
