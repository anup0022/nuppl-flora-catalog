Croton Garden Croton

Also known as: Croton / Garden Croton / Codiaeum

KingdomPlantae
FamilyEuphorbiaceae
GenusCodiaeum
SpeciesCodiaeum variegatum
Scientific nameCodiaeum variegatum
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Southeast Asia and Pacific Islands. Widely grown as ornamental across India
  • Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown in home gardens, pots, and nurseries like in your photo
  • Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate
  • Needs well-drained soil and bright indirect light to full sun. Color is brightest in good light
  • Evergreen shrub, 0.5-3 m tall. Leaves are leathery, glossy, and highly variable in shape and color. This variety shows broad leaves with yellow, green, and red/orange veining and blotches, which matches your photo

Uses

  • Used for hedging, borders, and container gardening. Over 100 cultivars exist with different leaf shapes and colors
  • Caution: Plant contains milky latex sap that is toxic and irritating. Can cause skin irritation and is poisonous if ingested. Note: Handle with gloves and consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use. Not for consumption
  • Commercial: High demand in nursery trade. Easy to propagate from cuttings

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental: One of the most popular foliage plants for gardens, landscaping, and indoor decor. No flowers needed - the colorful leaves are the main attraction
