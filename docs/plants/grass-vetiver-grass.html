Grasses Specimen

Vetiver grass

Also known as: Vetiver / Khus / Usheer / Vetiver grass

KingdomPlantae
FamilyPoaceae
GenusChrysopogon
SpeciesChrysopogon zizanioides
Scientific nameChrysopogon zizanioides
CategoryGrasses

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to India and widely distributed across tropical regions, found on roadsides, wastelands, riverbanks, and planted for soil conservation. Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate, extremely drought and flood tolerant, and grows in almost any soil, even poor, saline, or waterlogged soil, needing full sun. Forms dense clumps 1-2 m tall with long, thin, aromatic leaves growing upright from a central base; non-invasive as it doesn't spread by stolons, and roots can go 3-4 m deep.

Uses

  • Roots used to make khus mats, fans, and screens for evaporative cooling; dried grass used for handicrafts, thatching, and mulch; used in herbal sachets as a natural insect repellent.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Essential oil: Roots yield "khus oil" used in perfumery, cosmetics, soaps, and aromatherapy, with a cooling, earthy scent.
  • Important for soil and water conservation; deep roots prevent soil erosion and bind soil on slopes, and it is used in phytoremediation to clean polluted water and soil and to stabilise embankments.

Medicinal Importance

  • Roots used in Ayurveda for their cooling effect, for fever, skin diseases, and as a blood purifier; used to make khus sherbet and syrup for summer. Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use.

Field Notes

  • Synonym: Vetiveria zizanioides
