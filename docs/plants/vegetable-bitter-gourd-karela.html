Bitter Gourd Karela

Also known as: Bitter Gourd / Karela

KingdomPlantae
FamilyCucurbitaceae
GenusMomordica
SpeciesMomordica charantia
Scientific nameMomordica charantia
TypeAnnual climbing vine
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm tropical fields and gardens with fertile soil, sun, water, and trellis support.
  • Tendrilled vine, lobed leaves, separate yellow flowers, and ridged bitter fruits that turn orange when mature.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide nectar for bees and other pollinators.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Important vegetable, pickling crop, and traditional food plant.

Medicinal Importance

  • Used traditionally in food-based preparations; concentrated use may affect blood sugar and requires medical advice.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
