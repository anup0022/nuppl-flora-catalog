Vegetable Plants Specimen

Climbing Bean Pole Bean

Also known as: Climbing Bean / Pole Bean

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusPhaseolus
SpeciesPhaseolus vulgaris
Scientific namePhaseolus vulgaris
TypeAnnual climbing herb
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm fields and gardens with fertile soil and trellis support.
  • Twining stems, trifoliate leaves, pea flowers, and long pods containing beans.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers attract bees and roots fix nitrogen through symbiotic bacteria.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Important vegetable and pulse crop eaten as green pods or mature beans.

Medicinal Importance

  • Properly cooked beans are food; raw beans can be harmful and should not be consumed.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
