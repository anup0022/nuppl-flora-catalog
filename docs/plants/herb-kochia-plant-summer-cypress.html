Herbs Specimen

Kochia Plant Summer Cypress

Also known as: Kochia Plant / Summer Cypress

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAmaranthaceae
GenusBassia
SpeciesBassia scoparia
Scientific nameBassia scoparia (syn. Kochia scoparia)
TypeAnnual herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Eurasia; grown as an ornamental annual and found in dry disturbed soils and open fields.
  • Upright bushy form with narrow leaves turns red in autumn before the plant sets small seeds.

Biodiversity Value

  • Seeds feed granivorous birds and foliage provides cover for small insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Used as a seasonal hedge, ornamental bedding plant, and occasionally for soil cover.

Medicinal Importance

  • Traditional uses are reported in some regions, but self-medication is not advised.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
