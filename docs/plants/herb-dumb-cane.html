Dumb Cane

Also known as: Dumb Cane / Dieffenbachia

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAraceae
GenusDieffenbachia
SpeciesDieffenbachia seguine
Scientific nameDieffenbachia seguine
CategoryHerbs
IUCN Red ListLeast Concern (LC) — IUCN Red List LC

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical rainforests of Central and South America. Widely cultivated across India
  • Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown as indoor and shade house plant in pots like in your photo
  • Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate
  • Needs well-drained soil, partial to bright indirect light. Does not like direct harsh sun
  • Evergreen herb, 1-2 m tall. Leaves are large, broad, oval, with striking green and white/cream variegation. This matches your photo - large variegated leaves with mottled green and light green/white patches in a green pot

Biodiversity Value

  • Large glossy leaves and shade tolerance let it thrive as forest-floor ground cover in its native range.
  • Popular indoor foliage plant for low light, though sap is toxic if chewed and should be kept from children and pets.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental: One of the most popular indoor foliage plants. Used for interior decoration, offices, and shade gardens due to attractive foliage and low light tolerance
  • Air purifying: Good at removing indoor air pollutants like xylene and toluene
  • Commercial: High demand in nursery and interior landscaping trade. Easy to propagate from stem cuttings

Field Notes

  • Common cultivar in your photo: Dieffenbachia 'Camille' or 'Tropic Snow'
