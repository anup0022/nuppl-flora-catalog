Herbs Specimen

Joyweed ( Lal Mehndi )

Also known as: Brazilian Joyweed / Purple Joyweed / Lal Mehndi / Joy Weed

Scientific nameAlternanthera brasiliana
TypePerennial shrub, often grown as ornamental groundcover or hedge
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Brazil and South America. Now naturalized and very common as an ornamental across India, including UP
  • Thrives in hot, humid tropical and subtropical climate. Grows year-round in North India with protection from severe frost
  • Prefers full sun for best purple color, but tolerates partial shade. Leaves turn greener in deep shade
  • Grows in any well-drained soil - sandy, loamy, or clay. Tolerates poor soil and moderate drought once established
  • Planted in home gardens, parks, road dividers, and as borders or low hedges. Rarely found wild in your area - it’s cultivated
  • Recovers fast after pruning. May die back in harsh UP winters but resprouts from base in spring

Ecological & Economic Importance

  • Ornamental: Most common use. Grown for its striking dark purple/maroon foliage that provides year-round color. Used in landscaping, borders, edging, and as a hedge. Very popular in Kanpur gardens
  • Medicinal: In folk medicine, leaf paste applied on wounds, cuts, and skin infections. Used for stomach ailments in Brazil. Contains antioxidants. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
  • Ground cover: Dense growth suppresses weeds. Used on slopes and in beds where grass won’t grow
  • Dye: Leaves can yield a purplish dye, though rarely used commercially
  • Environmental: Attracts small pollinators. Low maintenance and drought-tolerant once established
