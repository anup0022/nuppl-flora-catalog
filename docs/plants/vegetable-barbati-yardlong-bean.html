Vegetable Plants Specimen

Barbati Yardlong Bean

Also known as: Barbati / Boda / Yardlong Bean

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusVigna
SpeciesVigna unguiculata
Scientific nameVigna unguiculata subsp. sesquipedalis
TypeAnnual climbing herb
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm-season crop grown on supports in sunny fertile gardens and fields.
  • Twining stems, trifoliate leaves, pale flowers, and very long tender pods.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers support bees and the legume improves soil nitrogen.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Popular vegetable eaten as tender pods and a useful smallholder crop.

Medicinal Importance

  • Nutritious food; beans should be properly prepared before eating.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
