Lollipop Climber Shivalingi

Also known as: Lollipop Climber, Shivalingi, Native Bryony, Striped Cucumber

KingdomPlantae
FamilyCucurbitaceae
GenusDiplocyclos
SpeciesDiplocyclos palmatus
Scientific nameDiplocyclos palmatus (L.) C.Jeffrey
CategoryVines / Climbers

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A vine found in rainforests and dry rainforests, thickets, monsoon forests, and lowland to upland disturbed areas.

Biodiversity Value

  • In Marathi it is called shivlingi because its seed resembles a lingam, giving it cultural and devotional significance in parts of India.
