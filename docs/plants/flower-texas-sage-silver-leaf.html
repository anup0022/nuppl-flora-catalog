Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Dry sunny gardens and hedges; prefers excellent drainage and low to moderate irrigation.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide nectar for bees and butterflies.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Drought-tolerant ornamental hedge and landscape plant.

Medicinal Importance

  • No medicinal use is recommended for this ornamental shrub.
