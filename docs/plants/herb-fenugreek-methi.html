Herbs Specimen

Fenugreek Methi

Also known as: Fenugreek / Methi

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusTrigonella
SpeciesTrigonella foenum
Scientific nameTrigonella foenum-graecum
TypeAnnual herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cool-season crop grown in fields and kitchen gardens on fertile, well-drained soil.
  • Trifoliate leaves, small pale flowers, and narrow pods containing hard yellow-brown seeds.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers support bees and the legume contributes nitrogen to soil through root symbiosis.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Leaves are eaten as methi and seeds are used as a spice, sprout, and food ingredient.

Medicinal Importance

  • Traditionally used in food-based remedies; high-dose use requires medical advice.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
