Wild Indigo

Also known as: Wild Indigo, Yellow False Indigo, Horseflyweed

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusBaptisia
SpeciesBaptisia tinctoria
Scientific nameBaptisia tinctoria (L.) R.Br. ex Ait.f.
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A herbaceous perennial native to eastern North America, found from Minnesota south to Florida, preferring dry meadows and open woodland. Multiple bushy stems reach 60-90 cm tall, with silver-green trifoliate leaves and yellow flowers borne in spikes 4-8 cm long.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Historically used as a source of blue dye by early American colonists as a substitute for true indigo, though yielding a paler colour; considered rare enough in parts of its range to be a protected species in some US states.
