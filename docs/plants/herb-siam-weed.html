Siam Weed

Also known as: Siam Weed, Jack in the Bush, Christmas Bush

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsteraceae
GenusChromolaena
SpeciesChromolaena odorata
Scientific nameChromolaena odorata (L.) R.M.King & H.Rob.
CategoryHerbs
IUCN Red ListLeast Concern (LC) — IUCN Red List LC

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A fast-growing, multi-stemmed perennial shrub native to the Americas (from Florida and Texas south through Mexico, the Caribbean and South America), introduced widely to tropical Asia and Africa where it has become a serious invasive weed. Grows up to 2.5 m tall in open areas, or up to 10 m as a creeper scrambling over other vegetation in shade; the hairy, glandular leaves give off a pungent aromatic odour when crushed.

Biodiversity Value

  • Regarded as one of the most problematic invasive weeds within protected rainforests in Africa and Asia, preventing regeneration of native tree species and increasing forest-edge flammability; various biological controls (moths, gall-forming flies) have been introduced to manage it.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Regarded as one of the most problematic invasive weeds within protected rainforests in Africa and Asia, preventing regeneration of native tree species and increasing forest-edge flammability; various biological controls (moths, gall-forming flies) have been introduced to manage it.

Medicinal Importance

  • Fresh leaf juice is widely used across Africa and Asia in folk medicine to stop bleeding and speed wound healing.
