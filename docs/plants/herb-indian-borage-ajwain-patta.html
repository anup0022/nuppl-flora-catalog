Indian Borage Ajwain Patta

Also known as: Indian Borage / Ajwain Patta / Mexican Mint / Karpooravalli / Patharchur

Visual record01 photographs
Scientific nameColeus amboinicus (also called Plectranthus amboinicus)
TypePerennial succulent herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Southern and Eastern Africa, now naturalized across India
  • Very common in home gardens and pots in Kanpur and North India
  • Prefers semi-shade to full sun and well-drained soil
  • Thrives in warm, humid conditions but tolerates dry spells
  • Grows easily from stem cuttings; often kept on balconies and kitchen gardens

Economic Importance

  • Culinary: Leaves have a strong ajwain-like flavor. Used in pakoras, chutneys, and herbal tea

Medicinal Importance

  • In Ayurveda and folk medicine, used for cough, cold, asthma, indigestion, and sore throat. Consult a healthcare professional before medicinal use
  • Essential oil: Leaves contain thymol and carvacrol, which have antimicrobial properties
  • Ornamental: Grown as an aromatic potted plant due to its attractive fuzzy leaves
  • Household: Acts as a natural mosquito repellent. Leaves rubbed on skin for mild insect bites
  • Commercial: Increasing demand in herbal products, syrups, and lozenges
