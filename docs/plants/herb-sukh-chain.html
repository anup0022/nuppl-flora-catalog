Herbs Specimen

Sukh Chain

Also known as: Sukh Chain, Khejri, Jand, Ghaf

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusProsopis
SpeciesProsopis cineraria
Scientific nameProsopis cineraria (L.) Druce
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A small thorny tree, 3-5 m tall, adapted to extremely arid conditions with rainfall as low as 15 cm annually, indicating the presence of a deep water table; native to the Middle East and the Indian subcontinent (Rajasthan, Punjab, Haryana, Gujarat) as well as the Arabian Peninsula, where it is known as ghaf. Leaves are bipinnate with small creamy-yellow flowers followed by pods.

Biodiversity Value

  • As a nitrogen-fixing legume, it enriches soil fertility, letting crops and grasses grow beneath its canopy in arid land.
  • A keystone species of Rajasthan's desert ecosystem, providing fodder, shade and nesting sites where little else survives.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Considered a vital multipurpose tree of arid regions, providing fodder, fuelwood and shade; pods (sangri) are used in traditional desert cuisine of the Thar Desert.

Medicinal Importance

  • This tree is highly revered in Hindu tradition and worshipped during the Dussehra festival; leaves are traditionally chewed to relieve mouth ulcers.
