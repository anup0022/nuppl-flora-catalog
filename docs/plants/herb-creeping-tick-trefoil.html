Creeping Tick Trefoil

Also known as: Creeping Tick Trefoil, Three-flower Beggarweed

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusGrona
SpeciesGrona triflora
Scientific nameGrona triflora (L.) H.Ohashi & K.Ohashi (
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical regions worldwide and introduced to subtropical regions including the southern United States.

Medicinal Importance

  • Used in folk medicine for wounds, diarrhea, rheumatism, dysentery and skin diseases; the whole plant has also been used in human nutritional treatment.
