Arbi Taro

Also known as: Arbi / Taro

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAraceae
GenusColocasia
SpeciesColocasia esculenta
Scientific nameColocasia esculenta
TypePerennial herb grown as a food crop
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm humid fields, wetlands, and gardens with rich moist soil; cultivars vary in water tolerance.
  • Large heart-shaped leaves arise from underground corms; flowering is uncommon in cultivated forms.

Biodiversity Value

  • Dense foliage provides shelter for small animals and moist-soil invertebrates.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Corms and leaves are important food crops when properly cooked.

Medicinal Importance

  • Raw corms and leaves contain irritating calcium oxalates and must be thoroughly cooked.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
