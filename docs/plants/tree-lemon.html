Lemon

Also known as: Lemon, Nimbu

KingdomPlantae
FamilyRutaceae
GenusCitrus
SpeciesCitrus x
Scientific nameCitrus x limon (L.) Osbeck
CategoryTrees

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A small evergreen tree, believed to have originated in northeast India as a natural hybrid of the citron and the bitter orange, from where it spread via Persia to the Mediterranean by the second century AD. Cultivated worldwide in tropical and subtropical climates; needs a minimum temperature of about 7 degrees C and is not frost-hardy.

Biodiversity Value

  • Fragrant flowers are an important nectar source for bees and butterflies.
  • Leaves serve as a larval host plant for citrus swallowtail butterflies.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • One of the most widely grown citrus fruits, cultivated globally for its juice, zest and pulp used in beverages, culinary dishes, marmalades, cleaning products and cosmetics; India and Mexico together account for around a third of world lemon and lime production.

Medicinal Importance

  • Juice used traditionally as a digestive aid, for sore throat, and as a vitamin C source supporting immunity.
