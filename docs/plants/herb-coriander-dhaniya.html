Herbs Specimen

Coriander Dhaniya

Also known as: Coriander / Dhaniya

KingdomPlantae
FamilyApiaceae
GenusCoriandrum
SpeciesCoriandrum sativum
Scientific nameCoriandrum sativum
TypeAnnual aromatic herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cultivated in kitchen gardens and farms during cool seasons; prefers full sun and fertile soil.
  • Soft divided leaves, small white flowers in umbels, and round aromatic fruits used as coriander seeds.

Biodiversity Value

  • Umbel flowers attract bees, hoverflies, and other beneficial insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Leaves and dried fruits are important culinary herbs and spices.

Medicinal Importance

  • Traditionally used as a digestive ingredient; medicinal doses should be professionally assessed.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
