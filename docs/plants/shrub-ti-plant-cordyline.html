Ti Plant Cordyline

Also known as: Ti Plant / Cordyline

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsparagaceae
GenusCordyline
SpeciesCordyline fruticosa
Scientific nameCordyline fruticosa
TypeEvergreen shrub
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical Asia and the Pacific; cultivated in warm gardens, containers, and shaded landscape beds.
  • Upright cane-like stems carry long lance-shaped leaves in green, red, or variegated forms; mature plants produce branched flower clusters.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers and berries can support insects and fruit-eating birds.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Popular ornamental foliage plant; leaves are used traditionally for wrapping and craft purposes.

Medicinal Importance

  • No medicinal use is recommended for this ornamental plant.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
