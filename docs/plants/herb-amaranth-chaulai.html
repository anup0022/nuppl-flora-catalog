Amaranth Chaulai

Also known as: Amaranth, Chaulai

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAmaranthaceae
GenusAmaranthus
SpeciesAmaranthus genus
Scientific nameAmaranthus sp. (genus Amaranthus L.)
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A genus of about 75 species of herbaceous annual and perennial plants with a cosmopolitan distribution across tropical regions, found from lowlands to Himalayan elevations. Plants grow 1-2.5 m tall with a succulent, often reddish stem and dense catkin-like flower clusters.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Widely cultivated worldwide as a leaf vegetable (called saag or cheera in India) and as a grain crop (amaranth grain, a gluten-free pseudocereal rich in protein, calcium, magnesium and dietary fibre). Ornamental species such as A. caudatus (love-lies-bleeding) are also grown for their long-lasting flower spikes.
