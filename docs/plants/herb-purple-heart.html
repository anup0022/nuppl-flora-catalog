Herbs Specimen

Purple Heart

Also known as: Purple Heart / Purple Queen / Wandering Jew

KingdomPlantae
GenusTradescantia
SpeciesTradescantia pallida
Scientific nameTradescantia pallida (syn. Setcreasea pallida)
TypePerennial evergreen herbaceous plant
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Eastern Mexico and the Gulf Coast
  • Now widely naturalized across tropical and subtropical India
  • Very common in gardens and pots in Kanpur and UP
  • Loves full sun for deep purple color, but handles partial shade
  • Grows in almost any soil as long as it drains well
  • Extremely heat and drought tolerant. Recovers fast from cuttings

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental: Top choice for its intense purple foliage. Used as ground cover, border plant, hanging baskets, and in balcony pots
  • Landscaping: Fast spreader, so used for mass planting and erosion control on slopes
  • Air purifier: Helps filter common indoor air pollutants

Medicinal Importance

  • In folk medicine, used for anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing properties. Speak to a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
  • Commercial: Huge in the nursery trade because it’s so easy to propagate from stem cuttings
  • Low maintenance: Thrives even with neglect, making it perfect for beginners
