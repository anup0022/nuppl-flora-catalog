Shrubs Specimen

Pencil Cactus Milk Bush

Also known as: Pencil Cactus, Milk Bush, Indian Tree Spurge

KingdomPlantae
FamilyEuphorbiaceae
GenusEuphorbia
SpeciesEuphorbia tirucalli
Scientific nameEuphorbia tirucalli L.
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Africa, growing in semi-arid tropical climates on black clay soils in northeastern, central and southern Africa; introduced widely to India, Brazil, Vietnam, the Philippines and Ghana. A succulent shrub or small tree up to 7 m tall with pencil-thick, cylindrical green branches and small oval leaves that fall off early; produces a milky, toxic and corrosive latex.

Biodiversity Value

  • Leafless, water-storing stems allow it to survive extreme drought on poor, dry soils.
  • Caustic latex deters browsing animals, letting it persist as a living fence in arid regions.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Grown as hedging and cattle fodder in dry savanna regions; its hydrocarbon-rich latex has been studied as a potential biofuel and rubber source, notably by the Brazilian national petroleum company in the 1980s.

Medicinal Importance

  • Used in traditional medicine across Brazil, India, Indonesia and Malaysia for ailments including asthma, cough, earache and rheumatism, although the latex is a skin and eye irritant and the plant's purported anticancer effects are not supported by rigorous research; contact requires protective gloves and eyewear.
