Peregrina Spicy Jatropha

KingdomPlantae

GenusJatropha
SpeciesJatropha integerrima
Scientific nameJatropha integerrima
TypeEvergreen flowering shrub
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm sunny gardens, hedges, and borders with well-drained soil.
  • Glossy leaves and clusters of bright pink to red five-petaled flowers borne over much of the warm season.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers attract butterflies and bees.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Ornamental hedge and flowering landscape shrub.

Medicinal Importance

  • Jatropha species may be toxic; do not ingest or use medicinally without expert identification.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
