Elephant Bush Dwarf Jade

Also known as: Elephant Bush / Dwarf Jade / Jade Plant

KingdomPlantae
FamilyDidiereaceae
GenusPortulacaria
SpeciesPortulacaria afra
Scientific namePortulacaria afra
TypeSucculent shrub
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to dry southern Africa; cultivated in sunny containers, rock gardens, and drought-tolerant landscapes.
  • Fleshy reddish stems carry small rounded succulent leaves; older plants develop a woody branching habit.

Biodiversity Value

  • Small pink flowers provide nectar for insects and dense branches offer shelter.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Hardy ornamental, bonsai subject, living hedge, and drought-tolerant landscaping plant.

Medicinal Importance

  • Leaves are eaten by some communities, but medicinal use should be professionally assessed.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Least Concern (LC) - IUCN Red List
