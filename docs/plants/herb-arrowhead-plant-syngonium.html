Arrowhead Plant Syngonium

Also known as: Arrowhead Plant / Syngonium

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAraceae
GenusSyngonium
SpeciesSyngonium podophyllum
Scientific nameSyngonium podophyllum
TypePerennial climbing herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical America; cultivated indoors and in shaded, humid gardens.
  • Juvenile arrow-shaped leaves change form as the vine matures; stems climb using aerial roots.

Biodiversity Value

  • Foliage provides cover for small invertebrates in humid planted habitats.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Popular ornamental foliage plant for containers, interiors, and vertical planting.

Medicinal Importance

  • Contains calcium oxalate crystals and is not safe to ingest.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
