Devil’s Backbone Zigzag Plant

Also known as: Devil’s Backbone / Zigzag Plant / Slipper Flower / Naagdon

Scientific nameEuphorbia tithymaloides (older name: Pedilanthus tithymaloides)
TypePerennial succulent shrub
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical Americas, from Florida to Venezuela
  • Widely grown across India as a garden and potted plant. Does very well in Kanpur’s climate
  • Prefers full sun to partial shade. Needs well-drained, sandy or loamy soil
  • Extremely drought tolerant. Hates waterlogging and frost
  • Often used as a hedge or border plant in hot, dry regions

Economic Importance

  • Ornamental: Popular for its unique zigzag stems and bright green foliage. Variegated varieties are common in landscaping
  • Hedge plant: Dense growth makes it useful for boundary hedges and living fences

Medicinal Importance

  • In folk medicine, latex is used for warts, skin issues, and earache. Leaves used for inflammation. Important: The milky sap is toxic and irritating to skin and eyes. Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
  • Cultural: Called “Naagdon” in India. Believed to keep snakes away, though not proven
  • Commercial: Sold widely in nurseries as a low-maintenance succulent shrub
