Arrowleaf Sida

Also known as: Arrowleaf Sida, Cuban Jute, Queensland Hemp

KingdomPlantae
FamilyMalvaceae
GenusSida
SpeciesSida rhombifolia
Scientific nameSida rhombifolia L.
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical and subtropical Africa, through India and Southeast Asia to Japan, Indonesia and the Philippines; introduced to the Americas, Australia, the Pacific and parts of Europe. An erect to sprawling, branched perennial or annual shrub-like herb 50-120 cm tall with diamond-shaped leaves; confined mostly to waste ground, roadsides and rocky areas, but can be competitive in pasture since livestock find it unpalatable.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • The stems yield a high-quality fibre once exported from India as "hemp", and are used for rough cordage, sacking and brooms. Leaves are notably nutrient-rich (protein, carbohydrate and fibre).

Medicinal Importance

  • Has significant traditional medicinal applications in India: pounded leaves are used to relieve swelling, fruits to relieve headache, the mucilage as an emollient, and the root to treat rheumatism. Used in Ayurvedic medicine more broadly.
