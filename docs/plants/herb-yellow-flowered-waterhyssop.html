Yellow-flowered Waterhyssop

Also known as: Yellow-flowered Waterhyssop, Baby Jump-up

KingdomPlantae
FamilyPlantaginaceae
GenusMecardonia
SpeciesMecardonia procumbens
Scientific nameMecardonia procumbens (Mill.) Small
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • An annual or perennial herb native to tropical and subtropical regions of the Americas, now naturalised in warmer regions on every continent except Antarctica, as well as on most islands with suitable climates.

Biodiversity Value

  • Small yellow flowers attract bees and hoverflies; low, spreading growth helps stabilise moist, disturbed soils.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Mainly of ecological rather than commercial significance, occasionally used as ground cover in damp garden beds.
