Cauliflower

KingdomPlantae
FamilyBrassicaceae
GenusBrassica
SpeciesBrassica oleracea
Scientific nameBrassica oleracea var. botrytis
TypeAnnual or biennial leafy herb
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cool-season vegetable crop in fertile, evenly moist soil.
  • Upright leaves surround a compact white, cream, or coloured head made of immature flower branches.

Biodiversity Value

  • Bolted plants provide yellow flowers visited by bees and other insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Widely cultivated vegetable eaten fresh, cooked, pickled, or processed.

Medicinal Importance

  • Nutritious food; medical claims should not replace clinical care.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
