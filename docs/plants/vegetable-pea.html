Vegetable Plants Specimen

Pea

KingdomPlantae
FamilyFabaceae
GenusPisum
SpeciesPisum sativum
Scientific namePisum sativum
TypeAnnual climbing herb
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cool-season fields and gardens with fertile soil, sun, and support for climbing varieties.
  • Compound leaves end in tendrils; white or pink pea flowers produce pods containing edible seeds.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers feed bees and root nodules fix nitrogen in soil.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Important vegetable and pulse crop eaten fresh, dried, or processed.

Medicinal Importance

  • Nutritious food source; therapeutic use requires evidence and medical guidance.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
