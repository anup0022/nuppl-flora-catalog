Vegetable Plants Specimen

Tomato

KingdomPlantae
FamilySolanaceae
GenusSolanum
SpeciesSolanum lycopersicum
Scientific nameSolanum lycopersicum
TypeAnnual or short-lived perennial herb
CategoryVegetable Plants

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Cultivated in sunny fields, gardens, and containers with fertile well-drained soil.
  • Branched aromatic stems, compound leaves, yellow flowers, and fleshy berries in many shapes and colours.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers provide pollen for buzz-pollinating bees.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Major vegetable and food crop eaten fresh, cooked, dried, and processed.

Medicinal Importance

  • A nutritious food source; unripe leaves and stems should not be eaten.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
