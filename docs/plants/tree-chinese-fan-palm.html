Trees Specimen

Chinese Fan Palm

KingdomPlantae
FamilyArecaceae
GenusLivistona
SpeciesLivistona chinensis
Scientific nameLivistona chinensis
TypeEvergreen palm
CategoryTrees

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to southern China, Taiwan, and Japan; cultivated in tropical and subtropical gardens, campuses, and streets.
  • Solitary trunk with large rounded fan leaves divided into drooping segments and persistent leaf bases.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers and fruits support insects and fruit-eating birds.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Popular ornamental and landscape palm; leaves are used locally for weaving and thatching.

Medicinal Importance

  • No established medicinal use is recorded for this cultivated specimen.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
