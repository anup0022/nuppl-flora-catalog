Asparagus Fern Foxtail Fern

Also known as: Asparagus Fern / Foxtail Fern

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsparagaceae
GenusAsparagus
SpeciesAsparagus densiflorus
Scientific nameAsparagus densiflorus
TypePerennial herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to southern Africa; cultivated in warm gardens, containers, borders, and shaded landscape beds.
  • Dense feathery green cladodes arise from tuberous roots; small white flowers may form red berries.

Biodiversity Value

  • Flowers attract small insects and berries may be eaten by birds, though the plant can spread outside cultivation.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Decorative foliage plant for borders, baskets, and floral arrangements.

Medicinal Importance

  • Berries should not be consumed; no medicinal recommendation is made here.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
