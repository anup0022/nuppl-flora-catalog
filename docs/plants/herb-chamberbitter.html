Chamberbitter

Also known as: Chamberbitter, Gripeweed, Shatterstone, Stonebreaker, Leafflower

KingdomPlantae
FamilyPhyllanthaceae
GenusPhyllanthus
SpeciesPhyllanthus urinaria
Scientific namePhyllanthus urinaria L.
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to Asia, now widely naturalized in tropical and subtropical regions worldwide including Mexico, Central and South America, Australia, Africa and the United States. A warm-season annual growing to about 60 cm, with small leaves that close at night; found on roadsides, disturbed land, forests, cropland and nurseries. Considered a competitive weed in some regions due to its abundant seed production, shade tolerance and extensive root system.
