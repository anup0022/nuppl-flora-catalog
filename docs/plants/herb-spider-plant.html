Spider Plant

KingdomPlantae
FamilyAsparagaceae
GenusChlorophytum
SpeciesChlorophytum comosum
Scientific nameChlorophytum comosum
TypePerennial herb
CategoryHerbs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Native to tropical and southern Africa; grown widely in homes, offices, pots, and shaded garden beds.
  • Tufts of narrow arching leaves produce long runners with small white flowers and plantlets.

Biodiversity Value

  • Small flowers provide limited nectar for insects.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Hardy ornamental foliage plant valued for hanging baskets and indoor landscaping.

Medicinal Importance

  • Not included as a medicinal plant in this catalog.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
