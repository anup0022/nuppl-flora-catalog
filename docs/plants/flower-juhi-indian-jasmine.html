Juhi Indian Jasmine

Also known as: Juhi / Indian Jasmine

KingdomPlantae
FamilyOleaceae
GenusJasminum
SpeciesJasminum auriculatum
Scientific nameJasminum auriculatum
TypeEvergreen flowering shrub or climber
CategoryFlowering / Ornamental

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • Warm gardens and hedges with fertile, well-drained soil and regular pruning.
  • Opposite leaves and intensely fragrant white flowers borne in clusters through warm seasons.

Biodiversity Value

  • Fragrance and nectar attract bees, moths, and other flower visitors.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Flowers are used in garlands, worship, perfumery, and ornamental planting.

Medicinal Importance

  • Jasmine preparations occur in traditional systems; use should be guided by a qualified practitioner.

Field Notes

  • Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
