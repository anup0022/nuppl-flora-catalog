Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Arjun is a large deciduous tree belonging to the family Combretaceae.
  • It grows well in moist, well-drained soils, especially near rivers and streams.
  • The bark is widely used in Ayurvedic medicine for heart health.
  • It helps reduce soil erosion because of its strong root system.
  • The tree provides shade and improves the environment.
  • Its wood is used for making agricultural tools and furniture.
  • The leaves serve as fodder for livestock.
  • It provides shelter for birds and other wildlife.
  • It is planted for afforestation and roadside beautification.
  • Arjun has high medicinal, ecological, and economic value.

Medicinal Importance

  • Bark decoction (Arjunarishta) is a classical Ayurvedic cardiac tonic used for heart health and blood pressure.
  • Also used traditionally for wound healing and as an astringent.

Authentic Ayurvedic Reference

  • Known in classical Ayurveda as Arjuna; its bark is a well-documented Hridya (cardiac-supportive) dravya, still used today in the classical formulation Arjunarishta.
