karonda

KingdomPlantae
GenusCarissa

Scientific nameCarissa carandas L.
TypeShrub
CategoryShrubs

Habitat & Growing Conditions

  • A hardy, thorny evergreen shrub found in dry wastelands, scrub forests, roadsides, and cultivated areas in tropical and subtropical regions of India.

Biodiversity Value

  • It helps prevent soil erosion.
  • It provides food for birds and wildlife.

Economic & Ecological Importance

  • Karonda is a thorny evergreen shrub.
  • It grows well in dry and rocky soils.
  • The fruits are rich in vitamin C and iron.
  • Fruits are used to make pickles, jams, jellies, and juices.
  • It is planted as a live fence because of its sharp thorns.
  • The plant is drought-tolerant and requires little care.
  • It helps prevent soil erosion.
  • Its roots, leaves, and fruits are used in traditional medicine.
  • It provides food for birds and wildlife.
  • It has good commercial value due to its edible fruits.

Medicinal Importance

  • Roots, leaves and unripe fruit used in traditional medicine for indigestion and as a remedy for anaemia.
