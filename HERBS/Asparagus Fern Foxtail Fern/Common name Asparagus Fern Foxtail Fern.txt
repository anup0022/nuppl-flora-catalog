Common name: Asparagus Fern / Foxtail Fern
Scientific name: Asparagus densiflorus
Family: Asparagaceae
Type: Perennial herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to southern Africa; cultivated in warm gardens, containers, borders, and shaded landscape beds.
Features: Dense feathery green cladodes arise from tuberous roots; small white flowers may form red berries.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers attract small insects and berries may be eaten by birds, though the plant can spread outside cultivation.
Economic importance: Decorative foliage plant for borders, baskets, and floral arrangements.
Medicinal importance: Berries should not be consumed; no medicinal recommendation is made here.
