Common name: Sicklepod, Tora, Sickle Senna
Scientific name: Senna tora (L.) Roxb.
Family: Fabaceae

Habitat: Grows wild throughout most of the tropics and is considered a weed in many places, though its native range is thought to be Central America. Closely related and often historically confused with Senna obtusifolia.

Economic Importance: Its seeds (commonly called "tora" or "chakvad" seeds in India) have traditional use as a coffee substitute and in folk food and medicine preparations across South and Southeast Asia.
