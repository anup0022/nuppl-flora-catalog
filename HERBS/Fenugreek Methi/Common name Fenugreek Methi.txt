Common name: Fenugreek / Methi
Scientific name: Trigonella foenum-graecum
Family: Fabaceae
Type: Annual herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season crop grown in fields and kitchen gardens on fertile, well-drained soil.
Features: Trifoliate leaves, small pale flowers, and narrow pods containing hard yellow-brown seeds.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers support bees and the legume contributes nitrogen to soil through root symbiosis.
Economic importance: Leaves are eaten as methi and seeds are used as a spice, sprout, and food ingredient.
Medicinal importance: Traditionally used in food-based remedies; high-dose use requires medical advice.
