Common name: Pineapple Plant
Scientific name: Ananas comosus
Family: Bromeliaceae
Type: Perennial fruit herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Tropical crop plant grown in warm sunny fields and home gardens with porous, well-drained soil.
Features: Stiff spiny rosette leaves surround a compact flowering stalk that develops into a crowned compound fruit.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide nectar for insects and the dense rosette offers small-animal shelter.
Economic importance: Important fruit crop used fresh, preserved, juiced, and processed into food products and fibre.
Medicinal importance: Fruit is nutritious, but concentrated preparations should be used with professional advice.
