Common name: Asthma Plant, Garden Spurge, Snakeweed, Tawa-tawa
Scientific name: Euphorbia hirta L.
Family: Euphorbiaceae

Habitat: A pantropical weed originating from tropical regions of the Americas; a hairy herb producing abundant white latex, growing in open grasslands, roadsides and pathways.

Medicinal Importance: Widely used in traditional herbal medicine across many cultures, particularly for asthma (giving rise to its common name), skin ailments and hypertension. In the Philippines it is consumed as an herbal tea known as tawa-tawa, particularly as a folk remedy for fevers including dengue fever.

Biodiversity Value:
- Tiny flower clusters attract ants and small pollinating insects; latex sap deters most grazing animals.

Economic & Ecological Importance:
- A widely used traditional remedy plant across the tropics, often deliberately left uncleared in gardens.
- Quick to colonise bare, disturbed ground, helping reduce soil erosion on paths and wasteland.
