Common name: Lily Foliage Plant, Peace Lily
Scientific name: Spathiphyllum sp. (genus Spathiphyllum Schott)
Family: Araceae

Habitat: A genus of 59 species of evergreen herbaceous perennials native to tropical regions of the Americas and southeastern Asia, growing on shady, moist forest floors along rivers and streams. Dark green, glossy elliptical to lanceolate leaves emerge in rosettes from a low creeping stem, with a distinctive white or greenish-white spathe surrounding the flowering spadix.

Economic Importance: Widely grown worldwide as a popular houseplant valued for its attractive dark foliage, contrasting white flowers and tolerance of low light and infrequent watering; commercially propagated by tissue culture for the interior plantscaping trade.

Biodiversity Value:
- Broad glossy leaves thrive in low light on shaded forest floors, and the species is noted for absorbing indoor air pollutants.
- White flower spathes attract pollinating insects in its native tropical habitat.
