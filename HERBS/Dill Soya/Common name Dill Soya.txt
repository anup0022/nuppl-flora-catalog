Common name: Dill / Soya
Scientific name: Anethum graveolens
Family: Apiaceae
Type: Annual aromatic herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cultivated in sunny beds and kitchen gardens in cool to warm seasons; prefers fertile, well-drained soil.
Features: Finely divided leaves, hollow stems, and yellow umbrella-shaped flower clusters produce aromatic seeds.
Biodiversity Value: Umbel flowers attract bees, hoverflies, and other beneficial insects.
Economic importance: Leaves and seeds are used as culinary herbs, pickles, and spice.
Medicinal importance: Traditionally used as a digestive herb; concentrated preparations require professional guidance.
