Common name: Yellow-flowered Waterhyssop, Baby Jump-up
Scientific name: Mecardonia procumbens (Mill.) Small
Family: Plantaginaceae

Habitat: An annual or perennial herb native to tropical and subtropical regions of the Americas, now naturalised in warmer regions on every continent except Antarctica, as well as on most islands with suitable climates.

Biodiversity Value:
- Small yellow flowers attract bees and hoverflies; low, spreading growth helps stabilise moist, disturbed soils.

Economic & Ecological Importance:
- Mainly of ecological rather than commercial significance, occasionally used as ground cover in damp garden beds.
