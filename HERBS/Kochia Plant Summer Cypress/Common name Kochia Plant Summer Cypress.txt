Common name: Kochia Plant / Summer Cypress
Scientific name: Bassia scoparia (syn. Kochia scoparia)
Family: Amaranthaceae
Type: Annual herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to Eurasia; grown as an ornamental annual and found in dry disturbed soils and open fields.
Features: Upright bushy form with narrow leaves turns red in autumn before the plant sets small seeds.
Biodiversity Value: Seeds feed granivorous birds and foliage provides cover for small insects.
Economic importance: Used as a seasonal hedge, ornamental bedding plant, and occasionally for soil cover.
Medicinal importance: Traditional uses are reported in some regions, but self-medication is not advised.
