Common name: Common Fleabane / Murva / Yellow Buttons  
Scientific name: Pulicaria angustifolia or Pulicaria crispa 
Note: Often also identified as Vicoa indica in UP region. All are closely related Asteraceae herbs with yellow button flowers
Family: Asteraceae

Habitat:
- Native to Asia and North Africa. Common across India
- Very common in Kanpur, UP area. Found in wastelands, crop fields, roadsides, and dry open areas
- Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate. Grows well in winter and monsoon
- Needs full sun. Grows in dry, sandy, loamy, and even poor soils. Drought tolerant
- Flowers mostly in winter to early summer. The yellow button-like flower heads are the key ID feature

Biodiversity Value:
- Flowers attract bees and beneficial insects.
- Helps in soil binding in wastelands

Economic importance:
- Medicinal: Main use in folk medicine and Ayurveda. Used for fever, cough, cold, skin diseases, and inflammation. Plant extract has anti-bacterial and anti-fungal properties. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
- Fodder: Eaten by goats and sheep. Used as dry fodder in some areas
- Other: Sometimes used as a minor green manure. Ash used in traditional practices

Medicinal Importance:
- Used in folk medicine and Ayurveda for fever, cough and skin diseases; extracts show antibacterial and antifungal activity.
