Common name: Arrowhead Plant / Syngonium
Scientific name: Syngonium podophyllum
Family: Araceae
Type: Perennial climbing herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to tropical America; cultivated indoors and in shaded, humid gardens.
Features: Juvenile arrow-shaped leaves change form as the vine matures; stems climb using aerial roots.
Biodiversity Value: Foliage provides cover for small invertebrates in humid planted habitats.
Economic importance: Popular ornamental foliage plant for containers, interiors, and vertical planting.
Medicinal importance: Contains calcium oxalate crystals and is not safe to ingest.
