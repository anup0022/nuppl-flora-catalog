Common name: Cattail / Bulrush / Patera / Paan / Elephant Grass 
Scientific name: Typha latifolia 
Common Indian species: Typha angustata and Typha domingensis are also common in India
Family: Typhaceae

Habitat:
- Cosmopolitan - found worldwide in wetlands
- Common in UP including Kanpur area in your coordinates. Grows in marshes, ponds, lakes, riverbanks, ditches, and waterlogged areas
- Thrives in freshwater to slightly brackish wetlands. Needs full sun
- Grows in standing water 0.3-1.5 m deep, or in very wet muddy soil
- Perennial herb, 1-3 m tall. Has long, flat, sword-like leaves and distinctive brown, sausage-shaped flower spikes like in your photo

Economic importance:
- Food: Young shoots, rhizomes, pollen, and immature flower spikes are edible. Rhizomes are rich in starch. Called "poor man's potato"
- Handicrafts: Leaves used for making mats, baskets, chair seats, and thatching roofs
- Medicinal: Rhizome and pollen used in traditional medicine for wounds, burns, and as diuretic. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
- Ecological: Excellent for wastewater treatment and wetland restoration. Absorbs pollutants and heavy metals. Provides habitat for birds and wildlife. Helps prevent soil erosion along water bodies
- Other: Fluffy seeds used as insulation and pillow stuffing. Dry leaves used as fuel and packing material
