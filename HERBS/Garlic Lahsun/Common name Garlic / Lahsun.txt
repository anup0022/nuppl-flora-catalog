Common name: Garlic / Lahsun
Scientific name: Allium sativum
Family: Amaryllidaceae
Type: Bulbous perennial herb grown as an annual crop
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cultivated in cool, dry-season fields and gardens with full sun and loose well-drained soil.
Features: Narrow strap-like leaves arise from a compound bulb; a mature scape bears a rounded cluster of small flowers and bulbils.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers, when produced, attract small pollinators.
Economic importance: Important culinary bulb, spice, preservative, and crop used worldwide.
Medicinal importance: Used traditionally as food and a household remedy; concentrated supplements may interact with medicines.
