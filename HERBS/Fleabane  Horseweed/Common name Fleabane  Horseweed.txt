Common name: Fleabane, Horseweed, Canadian Horseweed
Scientific name: Erigeron canadensis L. (syn. Conyza canadensis)
Family: Asteraceae

Habitat: Erigeron is a large genus of around 460 species in the daisy family with a cosmopolitan distribution, most diverse in North America. Erigeron canadensis (horseweed) is an erect, well-branched annual weed with small white to pale ray flowers, common on disturbed and cultivated ground, roadsides and waste places worldwide.

Economic Importance: Regarded mainly as a widespread agricultural weed; some other Erigeron species are cultivated as ornamental garden plants for their daisy-like flowers.

Biodiversity Value:
- Wind-dispersed seeds let it rapidly colonise bare and disturbed ground as an early pioneer species.
- Small flower heads provide late-season nectar and pollen for various insects.
