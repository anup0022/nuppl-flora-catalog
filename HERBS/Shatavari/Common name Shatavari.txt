Common name: Shatavari
Scientific name: Asparagus racemosus
Family: Asparagaceae
Type: Perennial climbing herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to South Asia; grows in scrub, woodland margins, and well-drained soils in warm climates.
Features: Slender climbing stems carry fine needle-like cladodes and produce fragrant white flower racemes; roots are fleshy and tuberous.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers support pollinating insects and the climbing growth contributes to understory diversity.
Economic importance: Cultivated and collected as an Ayurvedic medicinal herb.
Medicinal importance: Roots are traditionally used as a tonic, but dosage and identity must be confirmed by a qualified practitioner.
