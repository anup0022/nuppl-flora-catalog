Common name: Chamberbitter, Gripeweed, Shatterstone, Stonebreaker, Leafflower
Scientific name: Phyllanthus urinaria L.
Family: Phyllanthaceae

Habitat: Native to Asia, now widely naturalized in tropical and subtropical regions worldwide including Mexico, Central and South America, Australia, Africa and the United States. A warm-season annual growing to about 60 cm, with small leaves that close at night; found on roadsides, disturbed land, forests, cropland and nurseries. Considered a competitive weed in some regions due to its abundant seed production, shade tolerance and extensive root system.

Biodiversity Value:
- Tiny flowers under the leaves are insect-pollinated; seeds are explosively dispersed, aiding quick colonisation of bare soil.

Economic & Ecological Importance:
- Used in traditional medicine across the tropics; otherwise mainly a fast-spreading garden and nursery weed.
