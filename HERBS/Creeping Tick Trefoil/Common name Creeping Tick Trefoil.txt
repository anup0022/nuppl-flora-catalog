Common name: Creeping Tick Trefoil, Three-flower Beggarweed
Scientific name: Grona triflora (L.) H.Ohashi & K.Ohashi (synonym: Desmodium triflorum)
Family: Fabaceae

Habitat: Native to tropical regions worldwide and introduced to subtropical regions including the southern United States.

Medicinal Importance: Used in folk medicine for wounds, diarrhea, rheumatism, dysentery and skin diseases; the whole plant has also been used in human nutritional treatment.

Biodiversity Value:
- Small pea-like flowers attract bees; as a legume, its roots fix nitrogen and enrich surrounding soil.

Economic & Ecological Importance:
- Sticky, barbed seed pods cling to fur and clothing, aiding dispersal; used as low-grade fodder in some areas.
