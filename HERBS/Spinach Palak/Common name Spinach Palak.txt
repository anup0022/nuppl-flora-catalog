Common name: Spinach / Palak
Scientific name: Spinacia oleracea
Family: Amaranthaceae
Type: Annual leafy herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cool-season vegetable grown in fertile moist beds and fields with regular irrigation.
Features: Basal rosette of tender green leaves; mature plants produce slender flowering stems.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide pollen for small insects when plants are allowed to mature.
Economic importance: Widely cultivated leafy vegetable eaten fresh or cooked.
Medicinal importance: A nutritious food source; therapeutic use should not replace medical care.
