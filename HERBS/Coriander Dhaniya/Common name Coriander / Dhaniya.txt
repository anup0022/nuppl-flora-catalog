Common name: Coriander / Dhaniya
Scientific name: Coriandrum sativum
Family: Apiaceae
Type: Annual aromatic herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cultivated in kitchen gardens and farms during cool seasons; prefers full sun and fertile soil.
Features: Soft divided leaves, small white flowers in umbels, and round aromatic fruits commonly called coriander seeds.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide nectar and pollen for bees and other small pollinators.
Economic importance: Leaves and dried fruits are important culinary herbs and spices.
Medicinal importance: Traditionally used as a digestive ingredient; medicinal doses should be professionally assessed.
