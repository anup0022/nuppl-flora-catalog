Common name: Congress Grass / Gajar Ghas / White Top Weed  
Scientific name: Parthenium hysterophorus  
Family: Asteraceae

Habitat:
- Native to Mexico and Central America. Now one of the most widespread invasive weeds in India
- Very common in Kanpur, UP and across India. Found in wastelands, roadsides, railway tracks, crop fields, and vacant plots
- Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate. Grows in almost all soil types. Highly drought and heat tolerant
- Needs full sun. Grows fast in monsoon and winter. One plant can produce 25,000 seeds that survive in soil for years
- Considered a "noxious weed" because it spreads very aggressively

Economic importance:
- Negative - Major weed: Major problem for agriculture. Reduces crop yield by competing for nutrients, water, and light
- Health hazard: Pollen causes asthma, bronchitis, skin allergies, and dermatitis in humans and livestock. Avoid direct contact
- Toxic to animals: If eaten by cattle, it reduces milk yield and causes tainted milk. Can be fatal to livestock in large quantities
- Allelopathy: Releases chemicals that suppress growth of other plants and crops
- Limited uses: 
    - Used as green manure and compost after proper treatment
    - Extracts have some insecticidal and herbicidal properties being researched
    - Used in folk medicine in very controlled ways, but risky due to toxicity. Consult a healthcare professional before any use
