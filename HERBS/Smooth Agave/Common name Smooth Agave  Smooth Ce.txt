Common name: Smooth Agave / Smooth Century Plant / Agave desmetiana
Scientific name: Agave desmetiana
Family: Asparagaceae

Habitat: Native to Mexico, widely grown as an ornamental in India and common in urban gardens. Grown in pots, rock gardens, and nurseries. Thrives in hot, dry, tropical to subtropical climate, needing well-drained sandy soil, full sun, and being extremely drought tolerant. Forms a stemless succulent rosette, 60-90 cm tall and wide, with smooth, spineless, blue-green to bright green, strap-like leaves with soft flexible tips and no marginal teeth.

Biodiversity Value:
- Extremely low water needs, good for dry gardens and erosion control.

Economic Importance:
- Ornamental: Very popular landscape plant, used in xeriscaping, pots, gardens, and corporate landscaping; the smooth, spineless leaves make it safer for homes than other agaves.
- Other uses: Leaf fibres used for rope and handicrafts in native areas; some varieties used for making alcoholic beverages like agave syrup.
- Commercial: High demand in the nursery trade, propagating easily from pups/offsets.
