Common name: Spider Plant
Scientific name: Chlorophytum comosum
Family: Asparagaceae
Type: Perennial herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to tropical and southern Africa; grown widely in homes, offices, pots, and shaded garden beds.
Features: Tufts of narrow arching leaves produce long runners with small white flowers and plantlets.
Biodiversity Value: Small flowers provide limited nectar for insects.
Economic importance: Hardy ornamental foliage plant valued for hanging baskets and indoor landscaping.
Medicinal importance: Not included as a medicinal plant in this catalog.
