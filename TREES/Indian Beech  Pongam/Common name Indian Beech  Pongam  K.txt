Common name: Indian Beech / Pongam / Karanj / Honge  
Scientific name: Pongamia pinnata 
Synonym: Millettia pinnata
Family: Fabaceae / Leguminosae

Habitat:
- Native to India and Southeast Asia
- Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Found along roadsides, riverbanks, and planted in parks and campuses like in your photo
- Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate. Highly drought, flood, and salt tolerant
- Grows in almost any soil, even saline and waterlogged soils. Prefers full sun
- Medium to large deciduous tree, 8-15 m tall. Leaves are pinnate with 5-7 glossy, ovate leaflets like you can see. Young leaves are coppery-red

Economic importance:
- Biofuel: Seeds have 30-40% oil. Used for biodiesel production. Called "Honge oil"
- Medicinal: Bark, leaves, seeds, and oil used in traditional medicine for skin diseases, wounds, rheumatism, and as an antiseptic. Oil has antimicrobial properties. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
- Agriculture: Nitrogen-fixing tree. Leaves and seed cake used as green manure and organic fertilizer. Seed cake is also used as pesticide
- Other uses: Wood is used for agricultural tools and fuel. Oil used in soap making and as lamp oil. Tree is excellent for shade and soil conservation
- Ecological: Tolerates pollution and improves soil fertility. Provides shade and habitat for birds
