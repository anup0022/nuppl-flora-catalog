🌿 Name & Scientific Classification
Common Name: Neem

Scientific Name: Azadirachta indica

Family: Meliaceae

🌱 Growth Form & Habitat
Type: Evergreen tree, grows up to 15–20 m.

Habitat: Native to India; thrives in dry, arid, and tropical climates.

Features: Pinnate leaves with serrated leaflets, bitter taste, small white flowers, and green drupes (neem fruits).

💡 Economic & Medicinal Importance (8–10 lines)
Famous in Ayurveda and Unani medicine.

Leaves used for skin diseases, infections, and purification.

Neem oil extracted from seeds is a natural pesticide.

Bark used for dental care (neem datun).

Acts as a blood purifier in traditional medicine.

Widely used in cosmetics, soaps, shampoos.

Neem cake (seed residue) used as organic fertilizer.

Helps in mosquito and insect control.

Economically valuable in pharmaceutical and agricultural industries.

Culturally significant in India for rituals and health practices.
