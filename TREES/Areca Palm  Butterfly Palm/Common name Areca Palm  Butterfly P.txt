Common name: Areca Palm / Butterfly Palm / Golden Cane Palm
Scientific name: Dypsis lutescens (older name: Chrysalidocarpus lutescens)
Type: Evergreen palm, grown as an ornamental shrub or small tree

Habitat:
- Native to Madagascar
- Now widely cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions, including India
- Grows well in pots and gardens in Kanpur and North India
- Prefers bright indirect light, high humidity, and well-drained soil
- Sensitive to frost and direct harsh sun

Economic importance:
- Ornamental: One of the most popular indoor and landscape palms for homes, offices, and hotels
- Air purifier: NASA Clean Air Study lists it as effective at removing indoor toxins like xylene and toluene
- Landscaping: Used as hedge, screen, or accent plant in gardens
- Commercial: Major nursery trade plant; high demand in floriculture
- Humidity: Releases moisture, acting as a natural

Biodiversity Value:
- Clumping fronds create dense foliage recognised in air-quality studies as an effective indoor VOC remover.
- Provides year-round green cover and light shade, softening microclimates in courtyards and balconies.
