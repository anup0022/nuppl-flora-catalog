Common name: Chinese Fan Palm
Scientific name: Livistona chinensis
Family: Arecaceae
Type: Evergreen palm
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to southern China, Taiwan, and Japan; cultivated in tropical and subtropical gardens, campuses, and streets.
Features: Solitary trunk with large rounded fan leaves divided into drooping segments and persistent leaf bases.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers and fruits support insects and fruit-eating birds.
Economic importance: Popular ornamental and landscape palm; leaves are used locally for weaving and thatching.
Medicinal importance: No established medicinal use is recorded for this cultivated specimen.
