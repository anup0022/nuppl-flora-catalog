Common name: Norfolk Island Pine / Christmas Tree Pine / Araucaria  
Scientific name: Araucaria heterophylla  
Synonym: Araucaria excelsa  
Family: Araucariaceae

Habitat:
- Native to Norfolk Island, South Pacific. Widely cultivated across India
- Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown in pots, parks, and as an indoor/outdoor ornamental like in your photo
- Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate
- Needs well-drained soil and bright light to full sun. Tolerates coastal and urban conditions
- Grows 20-60 m tall in nature, but kept 1-3 m in pots. Branches grow in symmetrical whorls. This matches your photo - needle-like, soft green leaves arranged densely on branches with a pyramidal shape

Economic importance:
- Ornamental: One of the most popular ornamental conifers in India. Used as a Christmas tree, in pots, offices, and landscaping. Its perfect symmetry makes it very decorative
- Commercial: High demand in nursery trade. Sold as indoor plants and for festive decoration
- Timber: In native areas, wood is used for construction and boat building. Resin used for varnish
- Ecological: Good air purifier. Provides habitat for birds
