Common name: Bottlebrush / Tea Tree / Paperbark  
Scientific name: Melaleuca bracteata 
Often also called Melaleuca alternifolia for tea tree oil varieties  
Family: Myrtaceae

Habitat:
- Native to Australia. Widely cultivated across India
- Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown in gardens, parks, and as ornamental hedge
- Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate
- Needs well-drained soil and full sun. Highly drought and heat tolerant once established
- Evergreen shrub/small tree, 3-8 m tall. Leaves are narrow, needle-like, aromatic, and arranged alternately on drooping branches. New growth is bright lime green which matches your photo - fine, linear leaves on weeping branches

Economic importance:
- Medicinal: Leaves contain essential oil with antibacterial, antifungal, and antiseptic properties. Tea tree oil is used in skincare, soaps, shampoos, and medicines. Note: Consult a healthcare professional before medicinal use. Essential oil should not be ingested directly
- Ornamental: Popular for landscaping, screening, and avenue planting. Attractive weeping foliage and bottlebrush-like flowers
- Other uses: Wood used for small timber. Good windbreak and soil binder. Aromatic leaves used as natural insect repellent
- Commercial: High demand for tea tree oil in cosmetic and pharmaceutical industries
