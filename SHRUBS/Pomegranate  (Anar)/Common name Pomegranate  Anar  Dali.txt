Common name: Pomegranate / Anar / Dalim
Scientific name: Punica granatum
Type: Deciduous woody shrub or small tree

Habitat:
- Native to Iran, the Himalayas in northern India, and the Mediterranean region
- Extensively cultivated across India, especially in Maharashtra, Gujarat, UP, and Rajasthan
- Thrives in hot, dry climates like Kanpur. Very drought and heat tolerant
- Needs full sun - at least 6-8 hours daily for good fruiting
- Grows best in deep, loamy, well-drained soil but tolerates poor, alkaline, and slightly saline soils
- Cold hardy to light frost but sensitive to waterlogging

Economic importance:
- Fruit crop: Major commercial fruit in India. Arils eaten fresh, used in juice, syrups like grenadine, jams, and desserts
- Nutritional: Rich in antioxidants, vitamin C, potassium, and fiber. Juice is considered heart-healthy. Consult a healthcare professional before using for medicinal purposes
- Medicinal: In Ayurveda, rind, bark, and roots used for diarrhea, dysentery, and as an anthelmintic. Flower and leaf extracts have antimicrobial properties
- Industrial: Rind is a source of tannins for leather tanning. Peel and flowers yield natural dyes
- Ornamental: Grown in home gardens for fruit and attractive orange-red flowers. Dwarf varieties used as bonsai
- Cultural: Symbol of fertility and prosperity in many cultures. Important in traditional ceremonies
