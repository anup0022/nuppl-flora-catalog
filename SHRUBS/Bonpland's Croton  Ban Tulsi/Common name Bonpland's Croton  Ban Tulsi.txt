Common name: Bonpland's Croton, Ban Tulsi
Scientific name: Croton bonplandianus Baill.
Family: Euphorbiaceae

Habitat: A member of the large genus Croton within the spurge family, common as a weed of waste ground, roadsides and cultivated land across the Indian subcontinent. Like other Croton species, it is a hardy annual or short-lived shrub with simple alternate leaves and small inconspicuous flowers.
