Common name: Ti Plant / Cordyline
Scientific name: Cordyline fruticosa
Family: Asparagaceae
Type: Evergreen shrub
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to tropical Asia and the Pacific; cultivated in warm gardens, containers, and shaded landscape beds.
Features: Upright cane-like stems carry long lance-shaped leaves in green, red, or variegated forms; mature plants produce branched flower clusters.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers and berries can support insects and fruit-eating birds.
Economic importance: Popular ornamental foliage plant; leaves are used traditionally for wrapping and craft purposes.
Medicinal importance: No medicinal use is recommended for this ornamental plant.
