Common name: Yucca / Spanish Bayonet / Adam's Needle  
Scientific name: Yucca aloifolia  
Family: Asparagaceae

Habitat:
- Native to southeastern USA, Mexico, and Caribbean. Widely cultivated across India
- Very common in UP including Kanpur area at your coordinates. Grown in pots, gardens, nurseries, and along roadsides like in your photo
- Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate
- Needs well-drained soil and full sun. Extremely drought and heat tolerant
- Evergreen shrub, 1-3 m tall. Forms a woody trunk with age. Leaves are stiff, sword-shaped, narrow, and pointed with sharp tips. Arranged in dense rosettes at the top of the stem. This matches your photo - upright rosette of long, rigid green leaves with a visible central stem

Biodiversity Value:
- Good for dry gardens and soil stabilization

Economic importance:
- Ornamental: Very popular landscape plant. Used for xeriscaping, pots, garden borders, and corporate landscaping. Gives a strong architectural look with low maintenance
- Other uses: In native areas, roots contain saponins and were used as soap. Leaves used for fiber. Flowers are edible and used in salads
- Commercial: High demand in nursery trade. Easy to propagate from stem cuttings and pups/offsets
