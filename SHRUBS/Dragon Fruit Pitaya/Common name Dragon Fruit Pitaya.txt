Common name: Dragon Fruit / Pitaya
Scientific name: Selenicereus undatus
Family: Cactaceae
Type: Climbing cactus
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Tropical and subtropical gardens and farms; grows on supports in warm climates with porous soil and good drainage.
Features: Triangular succulent stems climb using aerial roots; large white night-blooming flowers develop into pink-skinned fruit.
Biodiversity Value: Night flowers provide nectar for moths and bats; fruits feed birds and mammals.
Economic importance: Commercial fruit crop and ornamental climber valued for edible pitaya.
Medicinal importance: Fruit is nutritious food; medicinal claims require appropriate clinical evidence.
