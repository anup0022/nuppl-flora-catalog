Common name: Elephant Bush / Dwarf Jade / Jade Plant
Scientific name: Portulacaria afra
Family: Didiereaceae
Type: Succulent shrub
Conservation Status: Least Concern (LC) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Native to dry southern Africa; cultivated in sunny containers, rock gardens, and drought-tolerant landscapes.
Features: Fleshy reddish stems carry small rounded succulent leaves; older plants develop a woody branching habit.
Biodiversity Value: Small pink flowers provide nectar for insects and dense branches offer shelter.
Economic importance: Hardy ornamental, bonsai subject, living hedge, and drought-tolerant landscaping plant.
Medicinal importance: Leaves are eaten by some communities, but medicinal use should be professionally assessed.
