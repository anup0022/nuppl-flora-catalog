Common name: Golden Duranta / Duranta / Shewari / Hedge Plant  
Scientific name: Duranta erecta 'Golden' / Duranta repens  
Family: Verbenaceae

Habitat:
- Native to tropical America. Widely planted as an ornamental in India
- Very common in UP including Kanpur area in your coordinates. Used in gardens, parks, schools, and as hedge plants like in your photo
- Thrives in tropical to subtropical climate. Prefers full sun to partial shade
- Grows in well-drained soils. Drought tolerant once established
- Evergreen, densely branched shrub, 1-3 m tall. Leaves are small, oval, glossy. New growth is bright yellow-green, older leaves are dark green - that's what you can see in your photo

Economic importance:
- Ornamental: Most popular hedge and border plant in India. Used for landscaping, fencing, and topiary because it takes pruning well
- Ecological: Provides nectar for butterflies and birds. Used for noise and dust barriers along roads
- Medicinal: Leaves and berries used traditionally for fever, skin diseases, and as an insect repellent. Note: Berries and leaves are toxic if ingested. Consult a healthcare professional before any medicinal use
- Other: Easy to propagate by cuttings. Low maintenance and pollution tolerant, so used a lot in urban areas
