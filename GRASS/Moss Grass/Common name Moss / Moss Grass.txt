Common name: Moss / Moss Grass
Scientific name: Bryum sp.
Family: Bryaceae
Type: Moss-forming non-vascular plant
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Moist shaded soil, stone, bark, and compacted surfaces where humidity remains high.
Features: Small leafy shoots form soft green cushions; spores develop in capsules on slender stalks.
Biodiversity Value: Moss mats retain moisture, provide microhabitat for invertebrates, and help stabilize fine soil.
Economic importance: Used as a natural texture in horticulture, terrariums, and ecological displays.
Medicinal importance: No medicinal use is recorded for this unidentified Bryum specimen.
