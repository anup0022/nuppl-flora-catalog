Common name: Rajnigandha / Tuberose
Scientific name: Agave amica (syn. Polianthes tuberosa)
Family: Asparagaceae
Type: Bulbous perennial herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm sunny gardens and flower fields with fertile, well-drained soil.
Features: Narrow basal leaves and tall spikes bearing waxy, intensely fragrant white flowers.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide nectar for moths, bees, and other pollinators.
Economic importance: Important cut flower used in bouquets, garlands, worship, and perfumery.
Medicinal importance: Fragrant flowers are used traditionally, but no self-medication is recommended.
