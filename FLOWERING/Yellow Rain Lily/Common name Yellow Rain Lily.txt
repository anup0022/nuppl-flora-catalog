Common name: Yellow Rain Lily
Scientific name: Zephyranthes citrina
Family: Amaryllidaceae
Type: Bulbous perennial herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Sunny lawns, borders, and pots; flowering often follows rain in warm climates.
Features: Narrow grass-like leaves arise from bulbs and bright yellow funnel-shaped flowers open singly.
Biodiversity Value: Open flowers provide nectar for small pollinating insects.
Economic importance: Popular low-maintenance ornamental for borders, rock gardens, and containers.
Medicinal importance: Bulbs should not be consumed; no medicinal use is recommended.
