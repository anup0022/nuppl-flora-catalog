Common name: Blanket Flower / Gaillardia / Indian Blanket
Scientific name: Gaillardia pulchella or Gaillardia × grandiflora
Type: Annual or short-lived perennial herb

Habitat:
- Native to North and South America, especially Mexico and southern US
- Widely grown across India as a winter/spring bedding plant. Does great in UP’s climate
- Loves full sun and open areas. Needs 6+ hours of direct sunlight for best flowering
- Prefers sandy, well-drained, poor to moderately fertile soil
- Extremely heat and drought tolerant. Blooms from March to June in Kanpur
- Does not tolerate waterlogging or heavy clay soil

Economic importance:
- Ornamental: Major seasonal flower in Indian gardens. Used in beds, borders, rock gardens, and pots. Flowers in shades of red, yellow, pink, and bi-colors
- Commercial: Seeds and saplings sold widely in nurseries. Low-cost, high-impact bedding plant
- Cut flower: Long-lasting blooms used in bouquets and floral arrangements
- Landscaping: Ideal for xeriscaping and low-water gardens. Used in public parks and road dividers
- Ecological: Rich nectar source. Attracts bees, butterflies, and beneficial insects
- Easy to grow: Self-seeds readily. One plant can produce hundreds of seeds, making it economical
