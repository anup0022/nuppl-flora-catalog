Common name: Chhoti Kamini / Hybrid Madhukamini
Scientific name: Murraya paniculata
Family: Rutaceae
Type: Evergreen flowering shrub
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cultivated in warm gardens and clipped hedges with sun to partial shade.
Features: Compact glossy foliage and repeated flushes of small fragrant white flowers.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers support bees and other small pollinators.
Economic importance: Used as a compact fragrant hedge and ornamental shrub.
Medicinal importance: No medicinal recommendation is made for this cultivated hybrid form.
