Common name: Garden Balsam / Gulmehndi
Scientific name: Impatiens balsamina
Family: Balsaminaceae
Type: Annual flowering herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Moist sunny or partly shaded garden beds and disturbed soils during the warm rainy season.
Features: Upright succulent stems, lance-shaped leaves, showy flowers, and explosive seed capsules.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide nectar for bees and butterflies.
Economic importance: Seasonal bedding plant traditionally grown in home gardens.
Medicinal importance: Traditional topical uses are reported, but safe dosage and identification require professional guidance.
