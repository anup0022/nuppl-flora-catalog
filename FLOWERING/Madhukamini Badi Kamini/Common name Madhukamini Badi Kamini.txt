Common name: Madhukamini / Badi Kamini
Scientific name: Murraya paniculata
Family: Rutaceae
Type: Evergreen flowering shrub
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm gardens, hedges, and roadsides in well-drained soil with sun or partial shade.
Features: Glossy compound leaves, dense branching, fragrant white flowers, and small red berries.
Biodiversity Value: Fragrant flowers attract bees, butterflies, and night-flying pollinators; berries feed birds.
Economic importance: Widely planted as a fragrant hedge, specimen shrub, and bonsai subject.
Medicinal importance: Traditional uses exist in some regions; this catalog does not recommend self-medication.
