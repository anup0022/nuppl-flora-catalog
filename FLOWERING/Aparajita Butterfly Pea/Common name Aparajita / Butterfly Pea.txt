Common name: Aparajita / Butterfly Pea
Scientific name: Clitoria ternatea
Family: Fabaceae
Type: Perennial climbing herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm open gardens and hedges; grows in full sun or light shade with support and moderate moisture.
Features: Twining stems, compound leaves, and vivid blue or white pea-shaped flowers followed by flat pods.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers attract bees and other pollinators; the legume fixes nitrogen.
Economic importance: Ornamental climber, traditional food and beverage colourant, and garden nitrogen fixer.
Medicinal importance: Used traditionally in herbal preparations; clinical and dosage advice is required.
