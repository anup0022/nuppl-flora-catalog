Common name: Moses-in-the-cradle / Boat lily / Oyster plant / Rhoeo
Scientific name: Tradescantia spathacea (older name: Rhoeo discolor)
Type: Perennial herbaceous plant

Habitat:
- Native to Central America, Mexico, and West Indies
- Now widely naturalized in tropical and subtropical regions including India
- Grows well in gardens, as ground cover, and in pots
- Prefers partial shade but tolerates full sun
- Needs well-drained soil and moderate watering

Economic importance:
- Ornamental: Popular garden and indoor plant due to green leaves with purple undersides
- Medicinal: In traditional medicine, used for colds, cough, sore throat, and wound healing
- Landscaping: Used as border plant and ground cover in parks
- Air purifier: Known to help remove indoor air pollutants
- Dye: Leaves sometimes used to produce natural purple dye

Biodiversity Value:
- Purple-and-green leaves and small white flowers draw minor pollinating insects; dense growth suppresses weeds.
- Tolerant of poor soil and shade, letting it thrive with little maintenance as border planting.
