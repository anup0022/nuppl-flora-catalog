Common name: Kalanchoe / Flaming Katy
Scientific name: Kalanchoe blossfeldiana
Family: Crassulaceae
Type: Succulent perennial herb
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Sunny windowsills, containers, and dry warm garden beds with excellent drainage.
Features: Fleshy scalloped leaves and dense clusters of red, pink, orange, yellow, or white flowers.
Biodiversity Value: Open flowers provide nectar for small insects.
Economic importance: Popular long-flowering pot plant and indoor ornamental.
Medicinal importance: Some Kalanchoe species are toxic; do not consume this ornamental plant.
