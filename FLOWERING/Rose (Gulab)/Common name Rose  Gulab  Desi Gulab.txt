Common name: Rose / Gulab / Desi Gulab
Scientific name: Rosa spp. This looks like Rosa indica or Rosa damascena
Type: Perennial flowering shrub

Habitat:
- Native to Asia, Europe, and North America
- Widely cultivated across India in gardens, pots, and farms
- Grows best in full sun and well-drained loamy soil
- Found in temperate to subtropical regions
- Common in home gardens, parks, and commercial farms

Economic importance:
- Ornamental: Most popular garden and cut flower worldwide
- Perfume industry: Rose oil and rose water made from Rosa damascena for attar and cosmetics
- Food: Petals used in gulkand, sharbat, sweets, and as garnish
- Medicinal: Rose water used for skin, eyes, and cooling properties in Ayurveda
- Religious: Flowers offered in temples, mosques, and used in ceremonies
- Commercial: Major floriculture crop; India is a key exporter of rose products

Biodiversity Value:
- Fragrant flowers attract bees, butterflies and other pollinators; hips are eaten by birds in autumn.
- Thorny growth provides safe nesting cover for small birds in hedges and garden borders.

Medicinal Importance:
- Rose water used in Ayurveda for its cooling effect on skin and eyes, and as a mild digestive aid.
