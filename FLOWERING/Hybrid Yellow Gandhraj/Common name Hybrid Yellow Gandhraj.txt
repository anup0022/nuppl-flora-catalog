Common name: Hybrid Yellow Gandhraj
Scientific name: Gardenia jasminoides (yellow hybrid cultivar)
Family: Rubiaceae
Type: Evergreen ornamental shrub
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Cultivated in warm gardens and containers with acidic fertile soil, moisture, and partial shade.
Features: Glossy dark leaves and highly fragrant double or semi-double yellow flowers.
Biodiversity Value: Fragrant flowers support bees and other pollinators.
Economic importance: Ornamental specimen, fragrant garden plant, and cut-flower subject.
Medicinal importance: Not included as a medicinal plant in this catalog.
