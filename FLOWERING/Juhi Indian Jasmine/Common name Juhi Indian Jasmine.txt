Common name: Juhi / Indian Jasmine
Scientific name: Jasminum auriculatum
Family: Oleaceae
Type: Evergreen flowering shrub or climber
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm gardens and hedges with fertile, well-drained soil and regular pruning.
Features: Opposite leaves and intensely fragrant white flowers borne in clusters through warm seasons.
Biodiversity Value: Fragrance and nectar attract bees, moths, and other flower visitors.
Economic importance: Flowers are used in garlands, worship, perfumery, and ornamental planting.
Medicinal importance: Jasmine preparations occur in traditional systems; use should be guided by a qualified practitioner.
