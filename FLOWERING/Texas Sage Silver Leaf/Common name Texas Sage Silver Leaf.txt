Common name: Texas Sage / Silver Leaf
Scientific name: Leucophyllum frutescens
Family: Scrophulariaceae
Type: Evergreen shrub
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Dry sunny gardens and hedges; prefers excellent drainage and low to moderate irrigation.
Features: Silvery leaves and purple to pink tubular flowers that often appear after rain or changes in humidity.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers provide nectar for bees and butterflies.
Economic importance: Drought-tolerant ornamental hedge and landscape plant.
Medicinal importance: No medicinal use is recommended for this ornamental shrub.
