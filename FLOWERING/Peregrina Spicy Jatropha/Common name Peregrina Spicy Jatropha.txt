Common name: Peregrina / Spicy Jatropha
Scientific name: Jatropha integerrima
Family: Euphorbiaceae
Type: Evergreen flowering shrub
Conservation Status: Not evaluated (NE) - IUCN Red List
Habitat: Warm sunny gardens, hedges, and borders with well-drained soil.
Features: Glossy leaves and clusters of bright pink to red five-petaled flowers borne over much of the warm season.
Biodiversity Value: Flowers attract butterflies and bees.
Economic importance: Ornamental hedge and flowering landscape shrub.
Medicinal importance: Jatropha species may be toxic; do not ingest or use medicinally without expert identification.
